# Mechanistic Materialism & Reasoning as Reckoning

## 1. Thesis & Definition

**Mechanistic Materialism and "Reasoning as Reckoning"** is the foundational philosophy originated by [[Thomas Hobbes|Thomas Hobbes]] in *Leviathan* (1651) and *De Corpore* (1655), representing the historical fountainhead of modern computational theories of mind and materialist cognitive science.

Hobbes formulated two interlocking theses:
1. **The Entire Mind is Mechanical (Matter in Motion)**: Sensation, imagination, memory, and passion are physical motions (*endeavors* and *phantasms*) inside the biological body and nervous system caused by physical contact with external objects.
2. **Only Reason is Computational (Addition and Subtraction of Names)**: Thinking in the strict sense of rational deliberation (*ratiocination*) is an arithmetical, rule-based procedure:
   > *"By Ratiocination, I understand computation. Now to compute is to collect the sum of many things added together at the same time, or to know the remainder when one thing is taken out of another. To ratiocinate, therefore, is the same with to add or to subtract... REASON, in this sense, is nothing but Conceiving, and reckoning of the consequences of generall names."* (*Leviathan*, Ch. 5; *De Corpore*, Ch. 1)

Modern cognitive scientist [[Jerry Fodor|Jerry Fodor]] summarized this legacy by observing that the foundational architecture of cognitive science is essentially **"Hobbes + Turing."**

## 2. Hobbes's Architecture of the Mind: Mechanical vs. Computational

A critical distinction in Hobbes's philosophy—often obscured in contemporary AI discourse—is that **computation is not the fabric of consciousness, but a specialized cultural and linguistic tool built on top of an embodied organism**:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      HOBBES'S FACULTIES OF MIND                        │
├──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Mental Faculty           │ Nature in Hobbes's Materialism              │
├──────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Sensation (Sense)     │ Physical pressure causing internal motions  │
│                          │ in the brain and heart (NOT computation).   │
├──────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Imagination & Memory  │ "Decaying sense" — lingering mechanical     │
│                          │ vibrations of matter (NOT computation).     │
├──────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Trains of Thought     │ Passive associative wandering driven by     │
│                          │ habit or desire (NOT computation).          │
├──────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Passions & Emotions   │ Internal physical drives/endeavors toward   │
│                          │ (appetite) or away (aversion) from objects. │
├──────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. Reason (Ratiocination)│ Deliberate, rule-based addition and         │
│                          │ subtraction of words and names (COMPUTATION)│
└──────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Why Reason Requires Speech (and Why Animals Have Minds without Computation)
For Hobbes, **animals possess minds**: a dog experiences sensations, memories, desires, fear, and even *prudence* (learning through association that thunder predicts rain). However, animals do not possess *Reason* because they lack speech—they cannot assign conventional names to abstract categories to perform formal addition (*"Man" + "Rational" = "Human"*) or subtraction (*"Body" - "Animation" = "Corpse"*).

## 3. The "Grand Inversion" of Modern AI

The nuance of Hobbes's architecture exposes what critics term **The Grand Inversion** in contemporary AI scaling:

```text
       NATURAL ORDER (Hobbes)                     THE GRAND INVERSION (Modern AI)
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ TOP: Symbolic Reckoning / Reason     │     │ TOP: Statistical Token Reckoning     │
│ (Deliberate linguistic tool)         │     │ (Automated matrix multiplications)   │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤     ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ BASE: Living, Embodied Organism      │     │ FALSE INFERENCE: Living consciousness│
│ (Metabolism, senses, feelings, pain) │     │ & sentience must emerge underneath!  │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘     └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Hobbes's Natural Hierarchy**: An embodied, feeling animal develops formal reckoning as a cultural tool on top of its biological drives.
2. **Silicon Valley's Inversion**: Engineers automate the top layer (statistical token reckoning and matrix mathematics) at extreme scale and speed, and then mistakenly infer that the biological base (phenomenal qualia, sentience, conscious access, moral patienthood) must have spontaneously emerged inside the silicon matrix.
   > *"They have automated the ledger and convinced themselves they have created the accountant."*
