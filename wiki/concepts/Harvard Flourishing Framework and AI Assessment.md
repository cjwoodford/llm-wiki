# The Harvard Flourishing Framework & AI Assessment

## 1. Thesis & Definition

**The Harvard Flourishing Framework**—developed by [[Tyler VanderWeele|Tyler J. VanderWeele]] and the [[Harvard Human Flourishing Program|Harvard Human Flourishing Program]]—defines flourishing as *"a state in which all aspects of a person’s life are good, including the contexts in which that person lives."*

Applied to artificial intelligence by VanderWeele & Jonathan Teubner (2026), the framework provides a multidimensional, normative blueprint for evaluating whether AI product design and user engagement promote or degrade authentic human flourishing across six universal domains of human life.

### The Four Communal Pathways to Flourishing
Empirical population studies identify four primary institutional engines of human flourishing:
1. **Family**
2. **Education**
3. **Work**
4. **Religious / Spiritual Community**

## 3. Five AI Considerations & The Non-Outsourceable Human Core

VanderWeele & Teubner establish five critical design and engagement considerations for AI:
1. **LLM Output Principles**: Directing responses toward human flourishing domains and redirecting vulnerable users back to face-to-face human counselors, pastors, and family.
2. **Product Design & Externalities**: Prohibiting products with clear negative externalities on human relationships.
3. **User Engagement & Opportunity Costs**: Tracking time trade-offs and preventing addictive engagement.
4. **Impact on Human Knowledge**: Guarding against hallucinations and epistemic degradation.
5. **The Non-Outsourceable Core of the Human Person**:
   > *"We cannot outsource human reason, human creativity, human relationships, human meaning, and human joy without ultimately giving up on the good life and human flourishing."*

### The Verdict on Relational Chatbots
The framework strongly advocates for **discontinuing all commercial relational and social chatbots for youth**, warning that synthetic intimacy creates counterfeit connections, distorts romantic expectations, and displaces genuine interpersonal development.
