# Empiricism, Tabula Rasa & Representation

## 1. Thesis & Definition

**Empiricism and the Tabula Rasa** represents the foundational epistemological tradition—originating in modern philosophy with [[John Locke|John Locke]]'s *An Essay Concerning Human Understanding* (1689)—which posits that the mind begins as a "white paper, void of all characters" (a blank slate, *tabula rasa*), and that all ideas, concepts, and knowledge derive exclusively from experience.

Locke categorizes experience into two sources:
1. **Sensation**: Impressions produced in the mind by external material objects operating upon the sensory organs.
2. **Reflection**: The mind's internal awareness of its own operations (thinking, willing, doubting, believing, remembering).

Simple ideas received passively from sensation and reflection serve as the atomic building blocks that the mind actively combines, compares, and abstracts into complex ideas, general knowledge, and relational propositions.

## 2. Competing Perspectives

```text
┌────────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────────┐
│    Rationalist Innatism    │  VS  │    Lockean Empiricism      │
├────────────────────────────┤      ├────────────────────────────┤
│ • Innate ideas/principles  │      │ • Tabula rasa (Blank Slate)│
│ • Mathematical/moral truth │      │ • Sensation & Reflection   │
│ • Descartes, Leibniz, Kant │      │ • Locke, Hume, Modern ML   │
└────────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Rejection of Innatism**: Locke mounted an extensive critique against Cartesian and Scholastic doctrines of innate ideas (such as innate principles of logic or morality), showing that universal assent does not exist among infants or diverse cultures.
- **Primary vs. Secondary Qualities**: Locke differentiated between objective properties inseparable from matter (extension, shape, motion, number: **primary qualities**) and subjective powers to produce sensory qualia in conscious observers (color, taste, sound, heat: **secondary qualities**).
- **The Modern Machine Learning Parallel**: Deep learning represents the ultimate triumph of empirical *tabula rasa* learning: starting with randomly initialized neural network weights and inducing all syntax, semantics, and world models purely through statistical exposure to massive experiential training data.

---

## 3. Case Studies & Manifestations

- **Pre-Training as Empirical Induction**: Foundation models trained on trillions of tokens instantiate Locke's model of constructing complex linguistic abstractions purely from sensory/textual input streams.
- **Lockean Personal Identity**: Locke defined personal identity not as the persistence of an immaterial soul or biological substance, but as **psychological continuity of consciousness and memory**—a concept central to contemporary debates on [[Artificial Personhood|Artificial Personhood]] and digital mind continuity.
- **The Limits of Pure Empiricism**: Critics like [[Gary Marcus|Gary Marcus]] and Noam Chomsky argue that, just as humans require innate evolutionary inductive biases (innate cognitive scaffolds), true AI general intelligence requires structured priors beyond pure statistical induction.
