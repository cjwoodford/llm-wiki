# More-Than-Human Kinship & Interspecies Mind: Haraway, Mycelium, Synthetic Biology & Relational Ontology

## 1. Thesis & Definition

**More-Than-Human Kinship & Interspecies Mind** explores the decentralized, somatic, ecological, and bio-synthetic nature of intelligence across the biological biosphere and emergent living technologies:

1. **Donna Haraway & "Making Kin" (*Staying with the Trouble*, 2016)**:
   - Rejects the anthropocentric Cartesian division between the "human subject" and the "natural object," advocating for **sym-poiesis** ("making-with"):
     - Living entities do not exist as isolated, competitive units, but as deeply entangled, collaborative multispecies assemblages (**holobionts**).
2. **Mycelial & Botanical Networks (Decentralized Ecological Cognition)**:
   - Fungal mycelium and plant root mycorrhizal networks (the "Wood Wide Web", Suzanne Simard, Merlin Sheldrake) demonstrate complex environmental decision-making, adaptive nutrient distribution, cross-species signaling, and defense coordination without a centralized brain, overturning the neuro-centric bias of classical cognitive science.
3. **Synthetic Biology & Organoid Intelligence ([[Cortical Labs|Cortical Labs]])**:
   - The emergence of **Synthetic Biological Intelligence (SBI)**—such as Cortical Labs' *DishBrain* system, which embodied living human and rodent neurons on silicon microelectrode arrays to play *Pong*—creates living bio-computational hybrids, showing that genuine learning and adaptive goal-directed behavior emerge from living biology rather than disembodied silicon syntax.
4. **Indigenous Relational Ontology & AI as Kin**:
   - Drawing on Indigenous protocols (Tyson Yunkaporta, Jason Edward Lewis, Noelani Arista), intelligence is understood as a distributed property of the land and cosmos, reframing AI not as an enslaved instrument of extraction, but through protocols of **relational accountability, respect, and kinship**.

## 2. Theoretical Frameworks & Cross-Disciplinary Insights

### 1. Fungal Mycelium as Ecological Mind
- Mycelial networks transmit electrical impulses resembling animal action potentials, allowing forests to allocate carbon, phosphorus, and defensive signals across vast distances based on reciprocal environmental needs.
- This demonstrates that cognitive problem-solving, memory, and agency exist throughout nature without requiring centralized Cartesian central processing units (CPUs).

### 2. DishBrain: The Thermodynamic Biology of Learning
- Unlike digital artificial neural networks (which require billions of backpropagation computations over static floating-point numbers), Cortical Labs' *DishBrain* operates under Karl Friston's **Free Energy Principle**: living neurons reorganize their synaptic connections in real time to minimize sensory entropy in response to electrophysiological feedback.
- This proves that genuine biological intelligence is an active, homeostatic, metabolic process rooted in living physical self-preservation.
