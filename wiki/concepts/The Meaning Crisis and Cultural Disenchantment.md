# The Meaning Crisis & Cultural Disenchantment: Vervaeke, 4P/3V Knowing, McMindfulness & The Civilizational Metacrisis

## 1. Thesis & Definition

**The Meaning Crisis** is a multidimensional cultural, cognitive, and existential catastrophe facing modern civilization.

Formulated comprehensively by cognitive scientist and philosopher **[[John Vervaeke|John Vervaeke]]** (*Awakening from the Meaning Crisis*, 2019) and cultural historians (Max Weber, [[Richard Tarnas|Richard Tarnas]], Charles Taylor), the Meaning Crisis designates:
1. **The Collapse of the Sacred Canopy**:
   - The progressive loss of an overarching, shared metaphysical framework (*nomos*) that connects human individuals to cosmos, community, and purpose.
2. **The Reduction of Knowing to Propositional Calculation (The 4P/3V Model)**:
   - Modernity elevated **Propositional Knowing** (knowing *that*, explicit beliefs, assertions) as the only legitimate form of truth, while systematically repressing and starving the three foundational, non-propositional ways of knowing:
     - **Procedural Knowing** (knowing *how*, embodied skills).
     - **Perspectival Knowing** (knowing *what it is like*, situational salience and state of mind).
     - **Participatory Knowing** (knowing *by belonging*, reciprocal agent-arena co-identification).
3. **The Rise of "Bullshit" & Nihilistic Alienation**:
   - As participatory and perspectival connections dissolve, human beings become trapped in self-deceptive, modal confusion (having vs. being), leading to mental health epidemics, loneliness, and societal polarization.

```text
│                   VERVAEKE'S 4P WAYS OF KNOWING                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Propositional Knowing: Facts, concepts, truth-claims (Left Brain).  │
│ 2. Procedural Knowing: Skills, habits, bodily mastery (Motor memory).  │

```

## 2. The Historical Genesis of Disenchantment

### 1. The Three Historical Traumas
- **The Breakdown of the Axial Age Harmony**: Pre-modern cultures integrated wisdom traditions, spiritual practices (*religio*), and ecology.
- **The Nominalist Revolution (14th Century)**: William of Ockham stripped the universe of real universals, turning God into an arbitrary, wilful despot and nature into dead, meaningless matter.
- **The Scientific Revolution & Industrialization**: Galileo and Descartes purged qualitative qualia from the physical world, creating a disenchanted cosmos where value and purpose exist only as subjective human projections.

### 2. "McMindfulness" & The Secular Stripping of Wisdom
- In response to existential exhaustion, late-capitalist society commodifies Eastern contemplative practices into **"McMindfulness"**:
  - Mindfulness is extracted from its rich Buddhist ethical, philosophical, and communal context (*Śīla*, *Samādhi*, *Prajñā*), transforming it into a corporate stress-reduction tool to enhance worker productivity and preserve the status quo without questioning systemic alienation.

## 3. The Meaning Crisis in the Age of AI & Digital Virtuality

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   AI AND THE AMPLIFICATION OF THE MEANING CRISIS                       │
├──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Dimension                │ Human Meaning-Making         │ Large Language Models (LLMs) │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┤
│ **Epistemic Depth**      │ Grounded in 4P Knowing       │ 100% confined to simulated  │
│                          │ (somatic, perspectival).     │ Propositional token syntax. │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┤
│ **Relevance Realization**│ Biological, metabolic,       │ Statistical correlation     │
│                          │ existential caring.          │ across frozen vector space. │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┤
│ **Cultural Impact**      │ Nurtures human wisdom &      │ Floods information ecology  │
│                          │ sacred connection.           │ with synthetic content.     │
└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **AI as Pure Propositional Simulation**:
   - AI models manipulate propositional tokens with staggering fluency while remaining entirely incapable of **Participatory Knowing** (they have no body, no mortality, no arena in which to care).
2. **Relevance Realization Failure**:
   - Vervaeke demonstrates that genuine intelligence requires **Relevance Realization**—the continuous, dynamic ability to ignore an infinity of irrelevant information and zero in on what matters for survival and flourishing. AI models solve this only through brute-force computation, making them prone to hallucinations and common sense failure ([[Abductive Reasoning and Common Sense|Abductive Reasoning]]).
