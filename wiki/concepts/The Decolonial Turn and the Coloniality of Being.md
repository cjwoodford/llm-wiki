# The Decolonial Turn & The Coloniality of Being: Epistemic Disobedience, Pluriversality & The Critique of Eurocentric Reason

## 1. Thesis & Definition

**The Decolonial Turn** is a foundational theoretical and philosophical movement initiated by Latin American, Caribbean, and Global South theorists—most notably **[[Aníbal Quijano|Aníbal Quijano]]**, **[[Walter Mignolo|Walter Mignolo]]**, Sylvia Wynter, María Lugones, and Enrique Dussel.

The core thesis establishes that while political colonialism (the direct administrative rule of European empires) formally ended, **Coloniality**—the underlying matrix of power, Eurocentric epistemology, ontological hierarchy, and racial capitalism—remains the governing operating system of global modernity:

1. **The Coloniality of Power (*Colonialidad del Poder*)**:
   - Formulated by Quijano (1992), showing how the conquest of the Americas invented the modern concept of "race" as a biological hierarchy to organize global labor, resources, and capitalist accumulation.
2. **The Coloniality of Knowledge (*Colonialidad del Saber*)**:
   - The violent universalization of Eurocentric rationality as the sole legitimate form of human knowledge, suppressing and delegitimizing Indigenous, African, and Asian cosmologies as "primitive superstition" (Epistemicide).
3. **The Coloniality of Being (*Colonialidad del Ser*)**:
   - The ontological dehumanization of non-Western peoples and the natural world, constructing the Western bourgeois subject (*Man 2*, in Sylvia Wynter's phrasing) as the universal definition of the human.
4. **The Pluriverse vs. The Universal**:
   - Decoloniality rejects the imposition of a single universal worldview ("a one-world world"), calling instead for a **Pluriverse**: *"a world in which many worlds fit"* (Zapatista formulation / Arturo Escobar).

## 2. Theoretical Frameworks & The Epistemic "Zero-Point"

### 1. The Hubris of the Zero-Point (Santiago Castro-Gómez & Walter Mignolo)
- Modern Western epistemology (tracing to Descartes' *cogito*) established the **"Hubris of the Zero-Point"** (*la hybris del punto cero*):
  - The philosopher pretends to observe reality from an invisible, detached, God-like vantage point that is outside of history, space, body, and culture.
  - [[Walter Mignolo|Mignolo]] exposes this as a colonial illusion: all knowledge is embodied, geopolitically situated, and historically located (*the geo-politics and body-politics of knowledge*).

### 2. Sylvia Wynter: The Overrepresentation of "Man"
- Jamaican philosopher Sylvia Wynter demonstrates how Western modernity secularized the Christian theological concept of the soul into **"Man 2"** (the economic, rational, self-maximizing actor, *Homo Oeconomicus*).
- This narrow historical construct is violently overrepresented as the universal archetype of all humanity, treating anyone who operates outside capitalist rationality as sub-human or deficient.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   DECOLONIALITY VS. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE MONOCULTURE                │

│                          │ more-than-human collective.  │ insensate training corpora. │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┤
│ **Concept of Human**     │ Pluriversal, embodied,       │ Computational agent /       │
│                          │ culturally diverse modes.    │ economic optimization model.│
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┤
│ **Power Dynamics**       │ Communal autonomy &          │ Centralized compute oligarchy│
│                          │ epistemic sovereignty.       │ & global data extraction.   │
└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **AI as the Digital Re-Inscription of the Coloniality of Knowledge**:
   - Modern foundation LLMs are trained primarily on digitized, English-dominant internet text, reflecting the linguistic, cultural, and political biases of the Global North.
   - Deploying these models globally as universal reasoning engines acts as a new form of **Algorithmic Colonialism**, flattening indigenous languages, oral knowledge systems, and local ontologies into a monocultural digital registry.
2. **Extractive Materialism**:
   - The physical infrastructure of AI (lithium mining in South America, cobalt extraction in the DRC, data-labeling sweatshops in the Global South, and massive water/energy consumption) replicates the classic extractive circuits of the Coloniality of Power.
