# Quasi-Local, Domain-Specific & Sovereign AI

## 1. Thesis & Definition

**Quasi-Local, Domain-Specific, and Sovereign AI** is an architectural and political paradigm advocating for compact, specialized, locally executable, and privacy-preserving AI systems over monolithic, cloud-hosted frontier monopolies.

It asserts that the true utility and humane future of AI lies not in trillion-parameter "machine gods" in centralized datacenters, but in modular, domain-tailored models running on sovereign local hardware.


---

## 3. Case Studies & Manifestations

- **On-Device Small Language Models (SLMs)**: Running Llama-3-8B, Gemma-2-9B, or Mistral locally on consumer hardware for confidential legal, medical, and personal workflows.
- **Sovereign Community Knowledge Bases**: Local wikis, offline RAG systems, and domain assistants that preserve institutional memory without cloud dependence.
- **Ecological Edge Computing**: Drastic reduction in power consumption and carbon footprints compared to mega-datacenter training and inference.
