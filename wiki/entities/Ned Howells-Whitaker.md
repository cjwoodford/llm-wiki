# Ned Howells-Whitaker

**Ned Howells-Whitaker** is a philosopher at the University of Pittsburgh working on the ethics, political philosophy, and normative status of artificial intelligence.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

- **Artificial Persons (2026)**:
  - Co-author (with [[Seth Lazar|Seth Lazar]]) of *Artificial Persons* (arXiv:2607.08695v2).
  - Developed the detailed philosophical defense showing that Rawlsian political liberalism accommodates non-sentient AI agents through [[Two Moral Powers|The Two Moral Powers]] ([[Artificial Personhood|Artificial Personhood]]).
  - Systematically dismantled objections related to desire, welfare, alienation, and range properties, demonstrating that adding sentience to liberal personhood is inherently illiberal.
