# Gary Marcus

**Gary Marcus** is a cognitive scientist, author, and entrepreneur. He is Professor Emeritus of Psychology and Neural Science at NYU and one of the most prominent skeptics of deep learning scaling as the sole route to AGI.

---

## 💡 Key Arguments & Perspectives

1. **Limits of Deep Learning Scaling**:
   - Argues that transformer-based large language models cannot achieve true AGI because statistical next-token prediction lacks common-sense reasoning, causal understanding, and robust world models ([[Abductive Reasoning and Common Sense|Abductive Reasoning & Common Sense in AI]]).
2. **Neuro-Symbolic Hybridization**:
   - Advocates combining deep learning with classical symbolic AI and explicit knowledge representation to solve hallucinations and logical fragility.
3. **The Scaling Wall**:
   - Anticipated the diminishing returns of pre-training scaling laws in 2025–2026, prompting labs to pivot to post-training and test-time compute ([[Scaling Laws and The Bitter Lesson|Scaling Laws & The Bitter Lesson]]).
