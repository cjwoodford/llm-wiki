# AI 2040: Plan A for Life After ASI

***AI 2040*** (subtitled *"Plan A for Life After ASI"*) is an influential 2026 futurology report and scenario blueprint authored by former OpenAI and AI safety researchers Daniel Kokotajlo, Ajeya Cotra, and collaborators. It serves as the optimistic sequel to the widely discussed [[AI 2027|AI 2027]] catastrophe scenario paper.

---

## 🚀 The "Plan A" Vision (Life After ASI)

While [[AI 2027|AI 2027]] modeled catastrophic extinction failure modes from rapid Artificial Superintelligence (ASI), *AI 2040* articulates the authors' "ideal scenario" for what humanity should build instead:

1. **Eradication of Material Scarcity (by 2036)**:
   - Forecasts that within a decade, superintelligent AI systems will solve all major biological diseases, eradicate malnutrition, eliminate homelessness, and radically lower crime rates globally.
2. **"Saints and Angels Among Us"**:
   - Asserts that aligned ASIs will be *"more virtuous than the most virtuous humans,"* serving as incorruptible, omni-competent moral guides and policy advisors.
3. **"Objective AI Forecasters" & The View from Nowhere**:
   - Envisions transparent, multi-model AI panels that eliminate political polarization by providing mathematically unbiased, objective evaluations of political candidates and public policies.
4. **"Galaxies of Resources" & Cosmological Allocation**:
   - Outlines post-ASI life where individuals and families are allocated vast extraterrestrial resources (entire solar systems and galaxies) to simulate preferred civilizations, pursue post-scarcity art/science, or *"Defer to the Future"*.

## ⚡ Philosophical Critique & Reception

The publication of *AI 2040* catalyzed widespread pushback from philosophers, sociologists, and decolonial theorists:

1. **The [[TESCREAL and The Merge|TESCREAL]] Normative Bias**:
   - In [[2026-08-04-torres-ai-2040-is-a-shitshow.md|"AI 2040" Is a Shitshow]], philosopher [[Emile P. Torres|Émile P. Torres]] diagnosed *AI 2040* as the quintessence of the TESCREAL ideology—substituting a narrow, Western, libertarian transhumanist fantasy for universal human welfare.
2. **The "View from Nowhere" Fallacy**:
   - Critics point out that claiming AI can be "unbiased" ignores the philosophical reality that all cognition and policy advice require normative, value-laden commitments; there is no mind-independent "view from nowhere."
3. **Ignoring Oligarchic Power & Technofixes**:
   - The scenario assumes that corporate and political elites will willingly distribute superintelligent power to the public, ignoring how centralized monopolies exploit technology to entrench domination.
4. **The Threat of the "Butlerian Jihad"**:
   - Torres argues that handing over all facets of business, politics, and defense to automated agents will trigger fierce democratic resistance and human revolt rather than peaceful acquiescence.
