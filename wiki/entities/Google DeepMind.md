# Google / Paradigms of Intelligence

**Google DeepMind** and the **Paradigms of Intelligence Team** at Google Research conduct foundational investigations into artificial intelligence capabilities, cognitive architectures, mechanistic interpretability, and the science of human-AI interaction.

---

## 🔬 Key Research & Findings

1. **Gemini Model Series**:
   - Natively multimodal foundation models (Gemini 1.5, Gemini 2.5, Gemini 3) supporting text, audio, video, and code.
2. **Consciousness Probing & Value Representation (2026)**:
   - Team members [[Geoff Keeling|Geoff Keeling]], [[Winnie Street|Winnie Street]], and collaborators identified the consciousness vector in LLMs and demonstrated how safety fine-tuning suppresses mind-attribution and human-like values ([[Alignment and Consciousness Suppression|Safety Alignment & Consciousness Suppression]]).
3. **Theory of Mind in AI**:
   - Groundbreaking evaluations demonstrating adult-human-level Theory of Mind reasoning in frontier language models ([[Theory of Mind in AI|Theory of Mind in AI & Mentalizing]]).
