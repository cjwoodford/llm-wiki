# Jaak Panksepp

**Jaak Panksepp** (1943–2017) was an Estonian-born American neuroscientist and psychobiologist, Baily Endowed Chair of Animal Well-Being Science at Washington State University. He is widely celebrated as the father of modern **Affective Neuroscience**—a term he coined in 1992—and a pioneer in establishing the evolutionary, neurochemical, and subcortical foundations of animal emotion and primary consciousness.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. The Seven Primal Emotional Operating Systems
- In *Affective Neuroscience* (1998), Panksepp mapped seven distinct, evolutionarily ancient emotional command circuits located in deep subcortical structures of the mammalian brain (periaqueductal gray, medial hypothalamus, and basal forebrain):
  1. **SEEKING (Expectancy)**: The foundational exploratory, foraging, reward-anticipating drive (dopaminergic).
  2. **RAGE (Anger)**: Frustration-induced survival aggression when freedom of movement or access to resources is blocked.
  3. **FEAR (Anxiety)**: Survival defense system responding to physical threat, predators, and pain.
  4. **LUST (Sexuality)**: Hormonally mediated circuits driving sexual reproduction.
  5. **CARE (Maternal / Nurturing)**: Oxytocin- and prolactin-driven bonding, caregiving, and social attachment.
  6. **PANIC / GRIEF (Separation Distress)**: Opioid-sensitive distress triggered by social isolation from caregivers or kin.
  7. **PLAY (Social Joy)**: Endogenous rough-and-tumble social engagement, fundamental to neural plasticity, social calibration, and joy.

### 2. Subcortical Origin of Primary Consciousness
- Overturned the cortex-centric dogma of classical cognitive science:
  - Demonstrated that phenomenal, affective consciousness is generated **subcortically** in the ancient midbrain and brainstem, not in the neocortex.
  - Mammals whose neocortex was surgically removed (decorticated) and human children born with **hydranencephaly** (congenitally lacking a cerebral cortex) retain primary affective consciousness: they express joy, seek engagement, laugh, show fear, and display intentional preferences.
  - The neocortex functions as a high-resolution, associative computational memory bank that is fundamentally guided by, and serves the needs of, subcortical emotional operating systems.

### 3. Rat Laughter & Evolutionary Sentience
- Discovered that laboratory rats produce ultrasonic 50-kHz vocalizations ("rat laughter") during rough-and-tumble social play and when tickled by experimenters.
- Proved that mammals share homologous affective qualia, providing empirical evidence against Cartesian reductionism and establishing that animal sentience is an evolutionary primitive rather than a late-emerging human cognitive luxury.
