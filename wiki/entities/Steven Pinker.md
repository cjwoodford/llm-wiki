# Steven Pinker

**Steven Pinker** (born 1954) is a Canadian-American cognitive psychologist, psycholinguist, and Johnstone Family Professor of Psychology at Harvard University.

Author of *The Language Instinct* (1994), *How the Mind Works* (1997), *The Blank Slate* (2002), and *Enlightenment Now* (2018), Pinker is a prominent public defender of classical computationalism (CTM), [[Noam Chomsky|Chomskyan]] generative linguistics, evolutionary psychology, and Enlightenment rationalism, drawing widespread critique from decolonial, phenomenological, and post-humanist theorists ([[The Divided Brain and Hemispheric Lateralization|The Divided Brain]]).
