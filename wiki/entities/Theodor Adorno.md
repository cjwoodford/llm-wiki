# Theodor Adorno

**Theodor W. Adorno** (1903–1969) was a German philosopher, sociologist, musicologist, and leading figure of the **Frankfurt School** of Critical Theory.

Co-author with [[Max Horkheimer|Max Horkheimer]] of *Dialectic of Enlightenment* (1944) and author of *Minima Moralia* (1951) and *Negative Dialectics* (1966), Adorno is famous for diagnosing the rise of **Instrumental Reason** and the standardization of consciousness through the **Culture Industry** ([[The Commodification of Dissent and Critical Theory|The Commodification of Dissent]]).
