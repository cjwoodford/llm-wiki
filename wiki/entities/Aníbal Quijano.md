# Aníbal Quijano

**Aníbal Quijano** (1928–2018) was a Peruvian sociologist, political theorist, and principal architect of the **Decolonial Turn**.

He is world-renowned for formulating the foundational concept of the **Coloniality of Power** (*Colonialidad del Poder*), demonstrating how racial classifications were invented during the European conquest of the Americas to structure global capitalism, labor extraction, and Eurocentric hegemony ([[The Decolonial Turn and the Coloniality of Being|The Decolonial Turn]]).
