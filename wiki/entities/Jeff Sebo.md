# Jeff Sebo

**Jeff Sebo** is Clinical Associate Professor of Environmental Studies, Affiliated Professor of Philosophy, and Director of the [[NYU Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy|Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy]] at New York University.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Studying AI Welfare Empirically (2026)**:
   - Co-lead author (with [[Robert Long|Robert Long]]) of *Studying AI Welfare Empirically*, formulating the 3-dimensional framework (Questions, Entities, Evidence) and the Six Principles of AI Welfare Science ([[Empirical AI Welfare and Digital Minds|Empirical AI Welfare & Digital Minds]]).
2. **The Moral Circle (2025)**:
   - Authored *The Moral Circle: Who Matters, What Matters, and Why*, developing a precautionary approach to extending moral considerability to non-human animals and digital minds.
3. **Intersubstrate Welfare Comparisons**:
   - Pioneered normative methodologies for comparing welfare and suffering across biological and digital substrates.
