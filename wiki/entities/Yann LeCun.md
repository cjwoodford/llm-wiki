# Yann LeCun

**Yann LeCun** is Chief AI Scientist at Meta, Silver Professor of Computer Science at NYU, and co-recipient of the 2018 Turing Award (alongside Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio) for breakthroughs in deep convolutional neural networks.

---

## 💡 Key Arguments & Perspectives

1. **Skepticism of Autoregressive LLMs**:
   - Argues that autoregressive LLMs (predicting the next token) will never reach human-level general intelligence due to error accumulation and lack of physical reality models ([[Machine Metaphor|The Machine Metaphor]]).
2. **Joint Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPA) & World Models**:
   - Advocates for self-supervised learning in abstract representation spaces to learn predictive world models directly from video and sensorimotor interaction.
3. **Open-Source AI**:
   - Champion of open-weight frontier foundation models (Llama series), enabling decentralized and sovereign AI development ([[Quasi-Local Sovereign AI|Quasi-Local Sovereign AI]]).
