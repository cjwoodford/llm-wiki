# Toby Ord

**Toby Ord** (born 1979) is an Australian moral philosopher, Senior Research Fellow at the [[Future of Humanity Institute]] at the University of Oxford, and co-founder of **Giving What We Can** (2009). He is best known for authoring ***The Precipice: Existential Risk and the Future of Humanity* (2020)**, which popularized rigorous quantitative risk assessments of human extinction from advanced artificial intelligence, synthetic biology, and engineered pandemics.

Within the **[[The Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage and Frontier AI|Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage]]**, Ord served as the intellectual bridge that connected grassroots utilitarian giving to high-stakes Oxford existential risk research and frontier AI alignment policy.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Founding Giving What We Can (2009)**:
   - In 2009, Ord pledged to donate all of his income above £18,000 (roughly $28,000) to effective charities for the rest of his life, inspiring thousands to take the 10% pledge and establishing the philanthropic core of Effective Altruism alongside [[William MacAskill]].
2. ***The Precipice* & The 1-in-6 Risk Metric (2020)**:
   - In *The Precipice*, Ord surveyed all known threats to human civilizational survival across the coming century, estimating the total existential risk at **1 in 6** (the roll of a six-sided die).
   - Crucially, Ord concluded that natural extinction risks (asteroid impacts, supervolcanoes) are negligible (less than 1 in 10,000), while anthropogenic risks from unaligned artificial general intelligence are by far the largest single contributor, which he estimated at **1 in 10** over the next 100 years.
3. **The Concept of the "Precipice"**:
   - Argued that humanity is currently in a uniquely dangerous epoch where our technological power to destroy ourselves has vastly outpaced our moral and institutional wisdom to manage that power responsibly.
