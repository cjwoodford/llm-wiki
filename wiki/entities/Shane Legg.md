# Shane Legg

**Shane Legg** (born 1973) is a New Zealand-born computer scientist, AI safety pioneer, and co-founder of **[[Google DeepMind|DeepMind]]** (alongside [[Demis Hassabis]] and [[Mustafa Suleyman]]), where he serves as **Chief AGI Scientist**.

Legg played a critical hinge role in the **[[The Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage and Frontier AI|Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage]]**, directly connecting theoretical algorithmic intelligence and LessWrong rationalism to venture funding, leading to DeepMind's founding seed investment by [[Peter Thiel]] in 2010.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Historic Role

1. **Formal Definitions of Universal Intelligence (2007)**:
   - Completed his PhD in 2008 at IDSIA under **Marcus Hutter**, the creator of the **AIXI** mathematical formalism for universal algorithmic intelligence.
   - Authored *"Machine Super Intelligence"* (2008), which provided rigorous formal mathematical metrics for general intelligence and explicit warnings regarding the existential risks of superhuman AI systems.
2. **The 2010 Singularity Summit & Thiel Seed Funding**:
   - As an avid reader of [[LessWrong]] and active participant in existential risk circles, Legg attended the 2010 Singularity Summit organized by [[Eliezer Yudkowsky]]'s SIAI in San Francisco.
   - Yudkowsky introduced Legg and [[Demis Hassabis]] to venture capitalist **[[Peter Thiel]]**. Legg warned Thiel that AGI was feasible within decades and that runaway superintelligence posed an existential threat to humanity.
   - Convinced by Legg’s mathematical rigor and the existential stakes, Thiel provided DeepMind with its crucial **£1.5 million seed investment**, cementing DeepMind's explicit corporate mission: *"Solve intelligence, and then use that to solve everything else."*
3. **DeepMind Ethics Board & Acquisition Safeguards (2014)**:
   - When Google acquired DeepMind in 2014, Legg and Hassabis demanded the creation of an independent internal AI Ethics and Safety Board as a pre-condition of sale, reflecting their rationalist roots and commitment to AGI safety governance.
