# David Abram

**David Abram** (born 1957) is an American cultural ecologist, philosopher, and phenomenologist. He is the founder of the Alliance for Wild Ethics (AWE) and the author of seminal works bridging [[Phenomenology and Embodied Cognition|continental phenomenology]] with ecology, animism, and linguistic history.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. *The Spell of the Sensuous* (1996) & "The More-than-Human World"
- In *The Spell of the Sensuous: Perception and Language in a More-than-Human World* (1996), Abram coined the phrase **"the more-than-human world"** to reject the dualistic split between "human culture" and "nature":
  - Human society is not a detached observer examining an insensate environment; it is a **subset embedded within an animate, sentient earthly collective**.

### 2. Eco-Phenomenology: Merleau-Ponty in the Living World
- Drawing deeply on [[Maurice Merleau-Ponty|Maurice Merleau-Ponty's]] phenomenology of the lived body (*corps propre*), Abram demonstrated that perception is never a passive reception of data by a Cartesian mind, but an **active, reciprocal bodily dialogue with the sensory surround**:
  - To touch a tree or a river is simultaneously to be touched by it; perception is intrinsically participatory and animistic.
  - Reappraised **Animism** ("The New Animism") as an empirically grounded recognition of the uncanny, bodily sentience of non-human entities and ecosystems.

### 3. The Phonetic Alphabet & The Severing of Sensuous Mind
- Formulated a historical diagnosis of modern alienation linking language, consciousness, and technology:
  - In primary oral cultures, language and meaning are distributed throughout the land—voices and stories live in the wind, rock formations, and animal tracks.
  - The invention and proliferation of the **phonetic alphabet** severed this ecological reciprocity. By mapping phonetic characters purely to human vocal sounds rather than natural phenomena (unlike pictographs), language turned inward, becoming a self-referential loop of human marks.
  - This linguistic abstraction laid the psychological foundation for the [[Enlightenment Conflation of Mind with Reason|Cartesian mechanization of nature]] and modern digital disembodiment.

### 4. "The Commonwealth of Breath" (*Becoming Animal*, 2010)
- In *Becoming Animal: An Earthly Cosmology* (2010), Abram reclaimed the primordial unity of breath, wind, and mind (*psyche*, *pneuma*, *ruach*, *prana*), demonstrating that the atmosphere is the shared physical and phenomenological matrix uniting all living subjects.
