# Peter Thiel

**Peter Thiel** (born 1967) is an American billionaire venture capitalist, political theorist, and entrepreneur. He co-founded PayPal, Palantir Technologies, and Founders Fund, and was the first outside investor in Facebook. Thiel is a primary financial and ideological patron of the **[[The Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage and Frontier AI|Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage]]**, providing crucial early seed capital to [[Eliezer Yudkowsky]]'s SIAI/MIRI, [[Google DeepMind|DeepMind]], and [[OpenAI]].

### 1. Defeating Death & Anti-Stagnation
- Heavily influenced by Girardian mimetic theory and Leo Strauss, Thiel views Western civilization as trapped in technological stagnation since the 1970s—with rapid progress confined to bits (computers) while atoms (energy, transportation, longevity) languished.
- Thiel embraced Extropian and transhumanist ideals: funding Aubrey de Grey's SENS anti-aging research, arranging for his own cryonic preservation with Alcor, and founding the Seasteading Institute with Patri Friedman to build autonomous oceanic city-states beyond democratic regulation.

### 2. The Financial Backbone of Early AGI
- **SIAI / MIRI (2006–2010)**: Thiel became the primary philanthropic patron of [[Eliezer Yudkowsky]], financing the growth of the Singularity Institute for Artificial Intelligence and its annual Singularity Summits.
- **DeepMind (2010)**: At the 2010 Singularity Summit, Yudkowsky introduced Thiel to [[Demis Hassabis]] and [[Shane Legg]]. Convinced that AGI represented both supreme commercial leverage and catastrophic existential peril, Thiel wrote DeepMind's first major institutional check of £1.5 million.
- **OpenAI (2015)**: Following Google's 2014 buyout of DeepMind, Thiel joined Elon Musk, Reid Hoffman, and [[Sam Altman]] to pledge $1 billion to establish OpenAI as an open, non-profit counterweight to protect humanity from corporate AGI monopolization.
