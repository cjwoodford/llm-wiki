# Christof Koch

**Christof Koch** (born 1956) is an American neuroscientist, former president and chief scientist of the Allen Institute for Brain Science, and long-time collaborator of Francis Crick.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Neural Correlates of Consciousness (NCC)**:
   - With Francis Crick, pioneered the empirical search for the minimal neuronal mechanisms sufficient for specific conscious percepts.
2. **Adoption of Integrated Information Theory (IIT)**:
   - Co-developed IIT with [[Giulio Tononi|Giulio Tononi]], arguing that consciousness is a fundamental property of the cosmos tied to integrated causal power ($\Phi$).
3. ***Confessions of a Romantic Reductionist* (2012)**:
   - Explored the intersection of biophysics, IIT panpsychism, and human spirituality, prompting [[John Searle|John Searle's]] famous critique in *The New York Review of Books*.
