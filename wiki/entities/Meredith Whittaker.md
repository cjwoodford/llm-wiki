# Meredith Whittaker

**Meredith Whittaker** is an American technology researcher, executive, and activist. She is the President of the **Signal Foundation**, co-founder and former Faculty Director of the **[[AI Now Institute]]**, and former Senior Advisor on AI to the Chair of the Federal Trade Commission ([[Lina Khan]]). Prior to leading Signal, she worked for over 13 years at Google as a senior engineering manager, where she co-organized the historic 2018 Google Walkout.

### 1. The Cloud Monopoly Cartel & Infrastructural Centralism
Whittaker’s foremost contribution to the political economy of artificial intelligence is her systematic exposure of the **cloud infrastructure cartel**:
- Countering the Silicon Valley narrative that AI is a democratizing force, Whittaker demonstrates that modern deep learning is structurally tethered to unprecedented concentrations of capital, proprietary datasets, and specialized compute clusters.
- Because only a tiny handful of hyperscalers—primarily Microsoft, Amazon, and Google—possess the planetary-scale data centers, subsea fiber cables, and custom silicon required to train frontier models, artificial intelligence acts as a **monopoly consolidation engine**, entrenching Big Tech control over global digital infrastructure.

### 2. Demystification of the "AI Mind"
Whittaker forcefully rejects philosophical and marketing mystifications regarding machine sentience, emergent minds, and existential risk:
- She characterizes generative AI fundamentally as a **surveillance and resource-extraction apparatus**, optimized to monetize human culture and behavioral telemetry.
- She argues that apocalyptic debates over hypothetical AGI extinction serve as a calculated diversion, deliberately distracting the public and regulators from acute, ongoing corporate harms: labor exploitation, mass surveillance, environmental destruction, and antitrust foreclosure.

### 3. Tech Labor Organizing & The Google Walkout
In November 2018, Whittaker co-organized the historic **Google Walkout for Real Change**, leading more than 20,000 employees out of Google offices worldwide to protest corporate handling of sexual harassment, forced arbitration, military cloud contracts (Project Maven), and systemic inequality. The action sparked modern labor organizing across the tech sector.

### 4. Privacy, Signal & End-to-End Encryption
As President of the Signal Foundation, Whittaker oversees the world’s premier private communications network, fiercely defending end-to-end encryption against regulatory backdoors and corporate attempts to weaken cryptographic privacy under the guise of content scanning or AI moderation.
