# NYU Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy

The **Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy (CMEP)** at New York University, directed by [[Jeff Sebo|Jeff Sebo]], is an academic research center dedicated to advancing philosophical, scientific, and policy research on the ethical and political implications of animal minds and artificial digital minds.

---

## 🔬 Core Research & Initiatives

1. **Empirical AI Welfare Science**:
   - Co-published *Studying AI Welfare Empirically* (2026) with [[Eleos AI Research|Eleos AI Research]], formulating methodological standards for assessing consciousness, sentience, and agency in AI systems ([[Empirical AI Welfare and Digital Minds|Empirical AI Welfare & Digital Minds]]).
2. **The Moral Circle & Digital Minds**:
   - Researches how to expand our moral, legal, and political consideration to include both non-human animals and artificial intelligences.
3. **Intersubstrate Welfare & Ethics**:
   - Develops frameworks for comparing suffering and welfare across biological and synthetic entities.
