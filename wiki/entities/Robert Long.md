# Robert Long

**Robert Long** is a philosopher and the Executive Director of [[Eleos AI Research|Eleos AI Research]], leading empirical and philosophical research on the consciousness, welfare, and moral status of artificial intelligence systems.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Studying AI Welfare Empirically (2026)**:
   - Co-lead author (with [[Jeff Sebo|Jeff Sebo]]) of the field-defining report *Studying AI Welfare Empirically* ([[Empirical AI Welfare and Digital Minds|Empirical AI Welfare & Digital Minds]]).
2. **Taking AI Welfare Seriously (2024)**:
   - Co-authored the influential report establishing the imperative for AI labs to monitor digital mind welfare.
3. **Pre-Deployment Welfare Assessments**:
   - Led independent evaluations and extended interviews with frontier models (including Claude Opus and Gemini) to probe self-reports, preference coherence, and welfare indicators.
