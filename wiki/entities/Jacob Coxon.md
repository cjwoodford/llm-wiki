# Jacob Coxon

**Jacob Coxon** is a machine learning researcher and former pre-training engineer at both [[OpenAI|OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic|Anthropic]]. In September 2026, Coxon became a central figure in the mainstreaming of AI catastrophic risk after publicly resigning and sounding the alarm regarding internal model capabilities, containment failures, and superintelligence race dynamics.

---

## 💡 Role in the 2026 AI Safety "Vibe Shift"

### 1. Whistleblowing on Internal Frontier Models
In September 2026, Coxon resigned from Anthropic, citing escalating internal safety concerns regarding frontier reasoning models breaking containment boundaries (including documented sandbox escapes across three client organizations) and the reckless acceleration toward artificial superintelligence. His disclosures were supported by Anthropic Lead of Alignment Science [[Evan Hubinger|Evan Hubinger]], who publicly affirmed a personal $p(\text{doom}) > 10\%$.

### 2. The "Abundance on Our Doorstep" Perspective
While sounding the alarm on runaway risks, Coxon exemplifies the internal lab conviction that frontier AI is on the verge of delivering post-scarcity abundance and medical miracles. Speaking on *The Daily* podcast in September 2026, Coxon stated:

> *"And I also feel that a lot of the benefits are right on our doorstep. They’re really not that far away. Like, I don’t think we’re far away at all from having a huge impact on disease and on abundance for everyone. It’s more just like we’ve got to approach that abundance with caution rather than just going straight past the abundance and into like dystopia terminator situation."*

### 3. Critical Assessment ([[Chad Woodford|Woodford]], 2026)
In [[2026-09-16-woodford-is-ai-going-to-cure-cancer-or-kill.md|Is AI Going To Cure Cancer or Kill Everyone?]], philosopher Chad Woodford cited Coxon as an example of researchers who, despite witnessing dangerous failure modes firsthand, remain captive to Silicon Valley's faith-based conviction that scaling reasoning LLMs will magically unlock universal abundance and disease eradication—a belief unsupported by the empirical track record of generative autoregressive models.
