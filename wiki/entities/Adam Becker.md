# Adam Becker

**Adam Becker** (born 1984) is an American astrophysicist, science journalist, and historian of science. He is the author of *What Is Real? The Unfinished Quest for the Meaning of Quantum Physics* (2018) and ***More Everything Forever: How Radical Futurism Stole Our Present and Sold Us a Lie* (2025)**.

Becker is a leading critical historian of the **[[The Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage and Frontier AI|Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage]]**, whose work unmasks the historical roots, pseudoscientific assumptions, and political economy behind Silicon Valley's existential risk and AGI dogmas.

---

## 💡 Key Critical Contributions & Analysis

1. ***More Everything Forever* (2025)**:
   - Provides a comprehensive historical and sociological autopsy of how fringe 19th-century eugenics, 1980s space colonization fantasies, and 1990s Extropianism were sanitized into the elite academic philosophies of **Longtermism** and **Rationalism**.
   - Traces the direct transmission of ideas and donor capital from [[Max More]]'s Extropy Institute through [[Eliezer Yudkowsky]]'s [[LessWrong]] and [[Nick Bostrom]]'s [[Future of Humanity Institute|FHI]], demonstrating how mathematical formalisms were used to launder radical cyber-libertarian dogma into moral authority.
2. **The "Doom Trolling" Thesis (*Better Offline*, September 2026)**:
   - In a landmark collaborative appearance with [[Cal Newport]] on the investigative tech podcast *Better Offline*, Becker diagnosed the psychological and economic function of **"Doom Trolling"**:
     - *Psychological Crutch*: For rationalists and longtermists who spent decades preaching that superintelligence was an inevitable imminent divinity, hyper-inflated extinction probabilities ($p(\text{doom}) > 20\%$) protect their worldview from disconfirmation as models hit empirical scaling walls and reasoning plateaus.
     - *Corporate Smokescreen*: Catastrophic sci-fi panic functions as an ideological shield for frontier labs ([[OpenAI]], [[Anthropic]]). By focusing congressional and media attention on theoretical rogue superintelligence, hyperscalers preempt regulation of immediate harms: massive power and water grid exhaustion, antitrust monopolization, copyright infringement, and the deployment of unaccountable autonomous agent swarms.
