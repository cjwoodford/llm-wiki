# Giulio Tononi

**Giulio Tononi** (born 1960) is an Italian neuroscientist and psychiatrist who holds the David P. White Chair in Sleep Medicine at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. He is the originator of **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)**.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)**:
   - Proposed that consciousness is an intrinsic, fundamental property of any physical system possessing non-zero integrated information ($\Phi$).
   - Formulated the five phenomenological axioms (existence, composition, information, integration, exclusion) and their physical postulates ([[Scientific Theories of Consciousness|Scientific Theories of Consciousness]]).
2. **The Posterior Hot Zone & Sleep Science**:
   - Identified the posterior cortical hot zone as the primary anatomical substrate of conscious experience and pioneered perturbational complexity indices (PCI) to measure consciousness during anesthesia and coma.
3. **Philosophical Controversies**:
   - IIT implies informational panpsychism, famously critiqued by [[John Searle|John Searle]] in *Can Information Theory Explain Consciousness?* (2013) for circular reliance on observer-relative information.
