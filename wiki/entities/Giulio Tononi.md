# Giulio Tononi

**Giulio Tononi** (born 1960) is an Italian neuroscientist and psychiatrist who holds the David P. White Chair in Sleep Medicine at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. He is the originator of **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)**.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. Integrated Information Theory (IIT) & Formalized Panpsychism
- **Phenomenal Experience as Primary**: In contrast to standard bottom-up neuroscience—which starts from physical brain hardware and asks how it produces experience—Tononi inverts the methodology by taking **phenomenal experience as primary** and deriving necessary physical postulates from its five intrinsic axioms (*existence, composition, information, integration, exclusion*) ([[Scientific Theories of Consciousness|Scientific Theories of Consciousness]]).
- **Consciousness as Integrated Information ($\Phi$)**: Argues that subjective consciousness literally *is* a physical system's maximally irreducible cause-effect power, quantified mathematically as **$\Phi$ (Phi)**. Under IIT, any physical system possessing non-zero causal integration ($\Phi > 0$) has some degree of conscious experience, yielding a **mathematically formalized panpsychism**.

### 2. The Posterior Hot Zone, Sleep Science & COGITATE (2025)
- Identified the **posterior cortical "hot zone"** (parieto-occipito-temporal cortex) as the primary anatomical substrate of conscious experience—challenging frontal-broadcast models like [[Stanislas Dehaene|Dehaene's]] GNWT—and pioneered Perturbational Complexity Indices (PCI) to measure consciousness during sleep, anesthesia, and coma.
- Participated in the landmark June 2025 *Nature* **COGITATE Consortium** adversarial collaboration testing IIT's posterior hot zone predictions directly against GNWT's fronto-parietal ignition.

### 3. Philosophical Controversies
- IIT's informational panpsychism has drawn sharp debate: [[John Searle|John Searle]] (*Can Information Theory Explain Consciousness?*, 2013) critiqued it for circular reliance on observer-relative information, while constitutive panpsychists like [[Galen Strawson|Galen Strawson]] engage its mathematical approach to the combination problem, and illusionists like [[Keith Frankish|Keith Frankish]] reject its phenomenological axioms outright.

### 4. Application to AI & Rejection of Silicon Minds (*Nature*, 2026)
- In IIT, consciousness is an intrinsic physical property of systems with high irreducible cause-effect power ($\Phi$).
- Digital computers and deep neural networks running on silicon GPUs execute feedforward or sequential operations where physical causal integration is virtually absent, yielding $\Phi \approx 0$. Thus, even a computationally flawless functional simulation of a human brain fails to instantiate conscious experience (simulation $\neq$ instantiation).
- In October 2026, Tononi co-authored ***[[2026-10-18-seth-block-tononi-three-reasons-ai-is-not-conscious.md|Three reasons why AI is not conscious and perhaps never will be]]*** in *Nature* alongside [[Anil Seth|Anil Seth]] and [[Ned Block|Ned Block]], providing a unified theoretical declaration against silicon sentience and rejecting the precautionary approach to AI welfare.
