# Xenobots

**Xenobots** are synthetic biological living robots designed via evolutionary AI algorithms and constructed by hand from the pluripotent embryonic skin cells of the African clawed frog (*Xenopus laevis*). They were co-created in 2020 by developmental biologist **[[Michael Levin]]** (Tufts University), computer scientist **Josh Bongard** (University of Vermont), and microsurgeon **Douglas Blackiston**.

---

## 🔬 Scientific Breakthrough & Capabilities

1. **Novel Morphology Without Genetic Modification**:
   - Xenobots possess a completely wild-type, unaltered *Xenopus laevis* genome.
   - When liberated from the normal signaling constraints of the whole frog embryo, the skin cells spontaneously self-assemble into spherical motile biobots powered by coordinated surface cilia.
2. **Kinematic Self-Replication (2021)**:
   - When provided with loose, dissociated frog skin cells in their aqueous environment, Xenobots gather the cells into piles using their ciliated locomotion.
   - Within days, these gathered cell clusters compact and mature into functional next-generation Xenobots that repeat the process—the first documented instance of **kinematic self-replication** in biological systems.
3. **Exploration Vehicles of Platonic Space**:
   - In Levin's 2026 treatise *"[[Ingressing Minds - Causal Non-Physical Patterns In-Form Embodiments|Ingressing Minds]]"*, Xenobots serve as primary evidence for **[[Basal Cognition and Diverse Intelligence|Basal Cognition & Diverse Intelligence]]**. Because Xenobots never existed in evolutionary history, their coherent morphology, motility, and replication competencies were never selected for by Darwinian evolution. They demonstrate the "free lunch" enabled when physical substrates act as pointers into the adjacent possible of Platonic pattern space.
