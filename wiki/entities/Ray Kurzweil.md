# Ray Kurzweil

**Ray Kurzweil** (born 1948) is an American inventor, futurist, and Director of Engineering at Google. He is the preeminent popularizer of the **Technological Singularity**, championing the inexorable exponential growth of computation and the merger of human intelligence with synthetic superintelligence.

---

## 💡 Key Conceptual Frameworks

1. **The Law of Accelerating Returns (2001)**:
   - Argues that technological evolution is an exponential process where each technological generation accelerates the creation of the next, leading to an asymptotic explosion in capability.
   - Forecasted that by 2029, artificial intelligence will pass a valid Turing Test, and by 2045, human intelligence will merge with machine intelligence in a cosmological Singularity.

2. **Singularitarianism & Mind Uploading**:
   - Outlined six evolutionary epochs (*The Singularity Is Near*, 2005), culminating in the universe "waking up" as matter and energy are saturated with computational intelligence.
   - Treats biological mortality as a temporary engineering limitation, advocating cryonics, nanomedicine, and neural uploading.

3. **Involvement with Extropians, SIAI, and Silicon Valley**:
   - Frequent keynote speaker at [[Machine Intelligence Research Institute|SIAI Singularity Summits]] alongside [[Eliezer Yudkowsky|Eliezer Yudkowsky]] and [[Nick Bostrom|Nick Bostrom]].
   - Co-founded Singularity University in 2008 with Peter Diamandis, sponsored by NASA and Google, institutionalizing transhumanist accelerationism in Silicon Valley executive culture.

---

## ⚖️ Critical Examination

- Critiqued by cultural critics like [[Meghan O'Gieblyn|Meghan O'Gieblyn]] (*God, Human, Animal, Machine*) for constructing a secular sublimation of Christian eschatology—reimagining the Resurrection as digital mind uploading and the Millennium as the Singularity.
- Critiqued by [[Adam Becker|Adam Becker]] (*More Everything Forever*) and [[Émile P. Torres|Émile P. Torres]] as a pillar of the [[TESCREAL and The Merge|TESCREAL]] ideological complex.
