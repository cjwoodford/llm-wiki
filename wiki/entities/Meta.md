# Meta

**Meta Platforms, Inc.** (formerly Facebook, Inc.) is an American multinational technology conglomerate based in Menlo Park, California. In artificial intelligence, Meta is the foremost corporate champion of the **open-weights foundation model paradigm**, developing the **Llama** (Large Language Model Meta AI) series and housing Fundamental AI Research (FAIR), led by Chief AI Scientist Yann LeCun.

### 1. The Open-Weights Counter-Strategy (Commoditizing the Complements)
Meta's deployment of the Llama ecosystem represents a classic corporate strategy of "commoditizing the complement" (Joel Spolsky):
- While competitors like [[OpenAI]] and [[Google DeepMind]] attempt to build closed, paid API moats around frontier intelligence, Meta open-sources model weights (up to 405B parameters).
- By providing the global developer community, enterprise ecosystem, and researchers with state-of-the-art open models for free, Meta neutralizes competitor software margins while accelerating developer adoption on its open hardware and advertising platforms.

### 2. The Copyright Frontier: Pretraining on Shadow Libraries
To train Llama 1, 2, and 3 on trillions of tokens, Meta scraped massive text datasets, including controversial **unauthorized shadow libraries** (e.g., Books3):
- In *[[Kadrey v. Meta Platforms]]*, a class of book authors sued Meta for mass copyright infringement.
- In June 2025, federal Judge Vince Chhabria granted summary judgment to Meta on its fair use defense, holding that the authors failed to show concrete evidence that Llama outputs substituted for or diluted book sales. However, the court warned that Meta's fair use defense remains evidentiary-dependent and vulnerable in future actions.

### 3. Openness vs. Catastrophic Risk Debates
Meta’s aggressive open-weights policy has placed it at the center of fierce policy and existential risk battles:
- Closed-lab safety researchers ([[Anthropic]], [[Machine Intelligence Research Institute|MIRI]]) argue that releasing open model weights is irreversible, eliminating the possibility of post-deployment safety guardrails, kill-switches, or bioweapon evaluations.
- Meta and Yann LeCun counter that open-source models are essential for scientific transparency, geopolitical resilience, and preventing a totalitarian private corporate oligopoly over intelligence.
