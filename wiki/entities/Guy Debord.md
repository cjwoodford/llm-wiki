# Guy Debord

**Guy Debord** (1931–1994) was a French Marxist theorist, philosopher, filmmaker, and founding leader of the **Situationist International**.

Author of *The Society of the Spectacle* (1967), Debord demonstrated that late capitalism had transformed authentic lived experience into a continuous, pacifying stream of commodified images and representations ([[The Commodification of Dissent and Critical Theory|The Commodification of Dissent]]).
