# Future of Humanity Institute

The **Future of Humanity Institute (FHI)** was a multidisciplinary research institute at the University of Oxford, founded in 2005 by philosopher Nick Bostrom and operating until its closure in April 2024. FHI served as the global intellectual fountainhead for the academic study of **existential risk (x-risk)**, **transhumanism**, **Longtermism**, and the **foundations of AI safety**.

---

## 🏛️ Core Research Pillars & Intellectual Legacy

1. **Existential Risk & Astronomical Waste**:
   - Formalized the study of human extinction risks (from advanced AI, synthetic biology, and geoengineering). Nick Bostrom's 2003 paper *"Astronomical Waste"* argued that delaying space colonization and digital consciousness creation wastes vast potential cosmological value ($10^{52}$ potential lives), providing the cornerstone for **Longtermism** ([[TESCREAL and The Merge|TESCREAL & The Merge]]).
2. **The Simulation Argument (Bostrom, 2003)**:
   - Proposed the trilemma asserting that at least one of three propositions is true: (1) civilizations go extinct before technological maturity, (2) mature civilizations lose interest in running ancestor simulations, or (3) we are almost certainly living in a computer simulation.
3. ***Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies* (Bostrom, 2014)**:
   - Transformed AI safety from a fringe concern into an urgent international priority, introducing concepts like the *Orthogonality Thesis*, *Instrumental Convergence*, and the *Treacherous Turn*.
4. **Institutional Offshoots & Ecosystem**:
   - FHI incubated much of the modern Effective Altruism (EA) and AI safety ecosystem, directly influencing the Global Priorities Institute, the Centre for Effective Altruism, the Centre for the Study of Existential Risk (Cambridge), Open Philanthropy, and frontier lab safety teams at [[Anthropic|Anthropic]] and [[Google DeepMind|Google DeepMind]].

---

## 🛑 Closure & Retrospective (April 2024)

In April 2024, the University of Oxford officially closed the Future of Humanity Institute following years of administrative and governance friction with the Faculty of Philosophy. While the physical institute dissolved, its researchers dispersed across global think tanks, university departments, and AI labs, ensuring its ideological and analytical framework continues to dominate contemporary AI alignment, [[Techno-Utopianism|Techno-Utopianism]], and digital mind ethics.
