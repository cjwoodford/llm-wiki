# Noam Chomsky

**Noam Chomsky** (1928–present) is an American linguist, cognitive scientist, philosopher, and social critic. Often hailed as the father of modern linguistics, Chomsky played a pivotal role in the **Cognitive Revolution** by establishing formal **Generative Grammar**, overthrowing behaviorist psychology through his 1959 critique of B.F. Skinner, and classifying computational formal languages via the **Chomsky Hierarchy**.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Philosophical Positions

### 1. *Three Models for the Description of Language* & MIT (September 11, 1956)
- Presented his breakthrough paper at the **[[The 1956 Foundations of AI and Cognitive Science#The MIT Symposium on Information Theory (September 10–12, 1956)|MIT Symposium on Information Theory on September 11, 1956]]**.
- Proved that natural language syntax cannot be modeled as a finite-state Markov process or statistical stimulus-response habit.
- Formulated phrase-structure grammars and transformational rules ($S \rightarrow NP + VP$), establishing the mathematical basis of computational linguistics.

### 2. The Chomsky Hierarchy (1956–1959)
- Structured formal languages into a nested hierarchy based on the computational power required to parse them:
  - **Type-3 (Regular Grammars)**: parsed by Finite State Automata.
  - **Type-2 (Context-Free Grammars)**: parsed by Pushdown Automata (foundational for computer programming languages and compilers).
  - **Type-1 (Context-Sensitive Grammars)**: parsed by Linear Bounded Automata.
  - **Type-0 (Unrestricted / Recursively Enumerable Grammars)**: parsed by Universal Turing Machines.

### 3. The 1959 Review of Skinner & The Demise of Behaviorism
- Published his legendary review of B.F. Skinner's *Verbal Behavior* in *Language* (1959), widely cited as the decisive intellectual blow against behaviorism.
- **Poverty of the Stimulus**: Demonstrated that children acquire rich, recursive grammar rapidly despite being exposed to noisy, incomplete, and finite linguistic data—proving that human brains possess an innate, genetically determined **Language Acquisition Device (LAD)** and Universal Grammar.

### 4. Critique of Modern LLMs & Statistical AI
- Chomsky has strongly criticized modern deep learning models (such as GPT-4):
  - Argues that statistical next-token predictors simply recreate sophisticated behaviorist correlation engines without genuine explanatory competence.
  - Notes that while human children effortlessly acquire possible languages and reject impossible grammars, LLMs process possible and impossible languages with equal indifference, proving they lack true linguistic cognitive structure.
