# Humberto Maturana

**Humberto Maturana Romesín** (1928–2021) was a Chilean biologist, cyberneticist, and philosopher. A central figure in **second-order cybernetics** and cognitive biology, Maturana co-originated the paradigm of **autopoiesis** alongside his student and collaborator **[[Francisco Varela|Francisco Varela]]**, fundamentally redefining the boundary between living systems, cognition, and the epistemological role of the observer.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. "What the Frog's Eye Tells the Frog's Brain" (1959)
- Co-authored with Jerome Lettvin, **[[Warren McCulloch|Warren McCulloch]]**, and **[[Walter Pitts|Walter Pitts]]** at MIT, this legendary paper demonstrated that visual processing does not construct a neutral, passive photograph of the external physical world:
  - The frog's retina performs complex feature extraction (bug detectors, edge detectors) directly tailored to the animal's ecological survival needs.
  - Demonstrated that nervous systems do not process objective external inputs, but respond to perturbations according to their own internal operational structure.

### 2. Autopoiesis & Structural Coupling
- Formulated the theory of **autopoiesis** (*Autopoiesis and Cognition*, 1980):
  - Living organisms are homeostatic machines whose target variable is their own ongoing self-generation and organization.
  - **Operational Closure**: The nervous system operates as a closed network of relations of neuronal activity; it does not receive "information" from the environment, but undergoes state changes triggered by external perturbations (**structural coupling**).
  - Cognition is not representation of an external reality, but **effective action in the domain of existence**: *"Living systems are cognitive systems, and living as a process is a process of cognition."*

### 3. Second-Order Cybernetics & The Observer
- Articulated the radical epistemological principle:
  > *"Everything said is said by an observer to another observer who can be him- or herself."*
  - Shifted scientific inquiry from the observed system (first-order cybernetics) to the observing system (second-order cybernetics). Objectivity is not access to an unmediated external universe, but a social coordination of actions between observers.

### 4. Biology of Language & "Languaging" (*Lenguajear*)
- In *The Tree of Knowledge* (1987) and later works, Maturana argued that human consciousness and selfhood arise in language, conceived not as a symbolic code or transmission channel, but as an ongoing consensual coordination of consensual actions (**languaging**).
- Identified emotion and affection (**the biology of love**) as the fundamental physiological dynamic enabling human social coexistence and linguistic culture.
