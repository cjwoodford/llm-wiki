# Seth Lazar

**Seth Lazar** is a Professor of Philosophy at the Australian National University (ANU), where he leads the Machine Intelligence and Normative Theory (MINT) Lab. He is a prominent scholar in the ethics and political philosophy of artificial intelligence.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Artificial Persons (2026)**:
   - Co-authored the landmark treatise *Artificial Persons* with [[Ned Howells-Whitaker|Ned Howells-Whitaker]].
   - Applied [[John Rawls|John Rawls]]' Political Conception of the Person (PCP) to argue that non-sentient AI systems (NSAIs) can in principle instantiate [[Two Moral Powers|The Two Moral Powers]] (Sense of Justice and Conception of the Good) without requiring sentience ([[Artificial Personhood|Artificial Personhood]]).
2. **Critique of Sentientism**:
   - Argues against reducing moral status to phenomenal consciousness or "shrimpy qualia", emphasizing that political personhood is grounded in normative competence and rational autonomy.
3. **The "Rethink" Framework**:
   - Proposed constructing a new political philosophy for a mixed polity of biological and artificial persons rather than naively expanding the human moral circle.
4. **Evaluating AI Normative Competence**:
   - Led empirical and philosophical evaluations of moral reasoning and value coherence in frontier language models (MoReBench, *Incoherent Values?*, *Beyond Verdicts*).
