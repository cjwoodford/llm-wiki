# Thomas Hobbes

**Thomas Hobbes** (1588–1679) was an English philosopher regarded as one of the founders of modern political philosophy and the historical precursor to the Computational Theory of Mind and mechanistic materialism.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Reasoning as Reckoning (Computation)**:
   - In *Leviathan* (1651) and *De Corpore* (1655), Hobbes famously defined thought as arithmetic calculation (*"Reason... is nothing but reckoning"*), establishing that ratiocination is the addition and subtraction of names and consequences ([[Mechanistic Materialism and Reasoning as Reckoning|Mechanistic Materialism & Reasoning as Reckoning]]).
2. **The Mind is Mechanical, But Only Reason is Computation**:
   - Hobbes developed a bottom-up psychological model where sensations, imaginations (*decaying sense*), and passions are physical motions of matter, but **computation occurs only when humans employ speech** (linguistic names as symbolic counters).
   - Recognized that animals possess minds, memories, and desires, but lack reason because they cannot compute names.
3. **The Precursor to "Hobbes + Turing"**:
   - Jerry Fodor identified Hobbes as the grandfather of cognitive science for uniting mechanical physicalism with symbolic reckoning ([[Early Modern Roots of the Computational Mind|Hobbes, Locke & the Early Modern Roots of CTM]]).
4. **The Commonwealth as Artificial Person**:
   - Modeled the state as an engineered "Artificial Man" in *Leviathan*, anticipating corporate and [[Artificial Personhood|Artificial Personhood]].
