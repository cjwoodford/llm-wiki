# Better Offline

**Better Offline** is an investigative technology podcast hosted by tech critic and analyst Ed Zitron, focusing on corporate malfeasance, executive hype, and the political economy of the technology sector. 

---

## 🎙️ The Landmark Episode: "From Extropians to Frontier AI" (September 2026)

On September 18, 2026, *Better Offline* released an influential collaborative episode featuring science historian **[[Adam Becker|Adam Becker]]** (author of *More Everything Forever: AI, Transhumanism, and the Venture Capital Quest for the Singularity*, 2025) and computer science professor and author **[[Cal Newport|Cal Newport]]** (Georgetown University).

The episode conducted a forensic post-mortem on the ideological origins of the modern AI boom, tracing how 1990s fringe transhumanist subcultures came to dictate the governance, culture, and capital allocation of today's frontier AI labs ([[Google DeepMind|Google DeepMind]], [[OpenAI|OpenAI]], and [[Anthropic|Anthropic]]).

---

## 💡 Core Historical & Political Theses

### 1. The 40-Year Evolutionary Transmission Belt
Becker and Newport trace the chronological lineage of Silicon Valley's existential AI ideology across four distinct institutional phases:
- **Phase 1: 1980s–1990s Extropians**: [[Max More|Max More]] and the Extropy Institute mailing list codifying cryonics, Drexlerian nanotechnology, mind uploading, and cyber-libertarian techno-salvation.
- **Phase 2: 2000s Rationalism & Academic Transhumanism**: [[Eliezer Yudkowsky|Eliezer Yudkowsky]]'s "Friendly AI" pivot and SIAI ([[Machine Intelligence Research Institute|MIRI]]), [[Nick Bostrom|Nick Bostrom]]'s [[Future of Humanity Institute|FHI]] at Oxford, and the launch of [[LessWrong|LessWrong]] and HPMOR as recruitment funnels.
- **Phase 3: 2010s Venture Capital & Lab Genesis**: [[Peter Thiel|Peter Thiel]]'s seed funding of SIAI and DeepMind (via [[Shane Legg|Shane Legg]] and [[Demis Hassabis|Demis Hassabis]]), [[William MacAskill|William MacAskill]] and [[Toby Ord|Toby Ord]] steering Effective Altruism toward Longtermism, and the 2015 creation of OpenAI to counter Google's monopoly on the "digital godhead".
- **Phase 4: 2020s Hyperscaler Co-optation**: Anthropic's $500M+ Series B funding from FTX / Sam Bankman-Fried, the failed November 2023 OpenAI board coup, and the definitive triumph of hyperscaler commercial capital over ideological safety boards.

### 2. The Deconstruction of "Doom Trolling"
The episode systematically dismantles the high-$p(\text{doom})$ rhetoric promulgated by tech executives and alignment theorists, identifying three interlocking functions:
1. **The Psychological Crutch**: Allows software engineers and executives to frame commercial product development as an eschatological, world-saving cosmic mission.
2. **The Distraction Smokescreen**: Deflects public, journalistic, and regulatory attention away from immediate, material harms—including monopolistic market concentration, massive energy and water consumption by data centers, copyright infringement, and labor displacement.
3. **The Regulatory Moat**: Aligns corporate executives with state regulators around apocalyptic licensing regimes (e.g., compute thresholds, weight-sharing bans) that entrench incumbent hyperscalers while outlawing open-source alternatives.
