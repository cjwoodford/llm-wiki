# Walter Mignolo

**Walter Mignolo** (born 1941) is an Argentine semiotician and William H. Wannamaker Professor of Literature at Duke University.

He is one of the leading figures in decolonial theory, celebrated for his critiques of the Eurocentric "Hubris of the Zero-Point," his formulation of **Epistemic Disobedience**, and his advocacy for the **Pluriverse**—a geopolitical and philosophical paradigm rejecting the universalization of Western reason ([[The Decolonial Turn and the Coloniality of Being|The Decolonial Turn]]).
