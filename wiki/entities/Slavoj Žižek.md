# Slavoj Žižek

**Slavoj Žižek** (born 1949) is a Slovenian philosopher, cultural theorist, and Senior Researcher at the University of Ljubljana.

Known as the "Elvis of cultural theory," Žižek combines Lacanian psychoanalysis with Hegelian dialectics to analyze modern ideology, showing that late capitalism thrives on cynical distance and commodified dissent ([[The Commodification of Dissent and Critical Theory|The Commodification of Dissent]]).
