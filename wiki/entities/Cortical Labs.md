# Cortical Labs

**Cortical Labs** is an Australian synthetic biology and biocomputing company based in Melbourne.

They are known for developing **DishBrain** (2022)—a Biological Computer System that integrates living biological human and rodent neurons with silicon microelectrode arrays to perform goal-directed computation (such as playing *Pong*), pioneering the field of **Synthetic Biological Intelligence (SBI)** ([[More-Than-Human Kinship and Interspecies Mind|More-Than-Human Kinship & Interspecies Mind]]).
