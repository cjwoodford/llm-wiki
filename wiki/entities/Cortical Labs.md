# Cortical Labs

**Cortical Labs** is an Australian synthetic biology and biocomputing company based in Melbourne.

They are known for developing **DishBrain** (2022)—a Biological Computer System that integrates living biological human and rodent neurons with silicon microelectrode arrays to perform goal-directed computation (such as playing *Pong*), pioneering the field of **Synthetic Biological Intelligence (SBI)** ([[More-Than-Human Kinship and Interspecies Mind|More-Than-Human Kinship & Interspecies Mind]]).

### Basal Cognition & Hybrid Embodiments
Cortical Labs' work exemplifies the **[[Basal Cognition and Diverse Intelligence|Basal Cognition & Diverse Intelligence]]** paradigm pioneered by **[[Michael Levin|Michael Levin]]**. By demonstrating that in vitro neural cultures minimize sensory entropy via Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle without an intact animal body, DishBrain acts as an engineered hybrid "pointer" into cognitive problem spaces, bridging the divide between biological wetware and silicon computing.
