# Max More

**Max More** (born Max O'Connor, 1964) is a British-born philosopher, futurist, and foundational architect of modern **Transhumanism** and **Extropianism**. In 1988, he co-founded *Extropy: The Journal of Transhumanist Thought*, and in 1991 launched the **Extropy Institute**, establishing the email mailing lists that incubated Silicon Valley's futurist and rationalist subcultures. He subsequently served as CEO and President of the **Alcor Life Extension Foundation** (2011–2020).

More represents the historical taproot of the **[[The Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage and Frontier AI|Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage]]**, formulating the cyber-libertarian ethos that physical mortality, biological limitations, and the human form are engineering problems to be conquered via technology.

---

## 💡 Core Philosophy & Contributions

1. **Principles of Extropy (1990)**:
   - Coined "Extropy" (as the conceptual opposite of entropy) to define the capacity of an intelligent system to continuously increase in order, complexity, information, and life.
   - Formalized the **Principles of Extropy**: Boundless Expansion, Self-Transformation, Dynamic Optimism, Intelligent Technology, Spontaneous Order (free markets and decentralized self-organization), and Rational Thinking.
2. **The Extropian Mailing Lists (1991–1996)**:
   - Created the vibrant, anarcho-capitalist online forum where pioneers of cryptography (cypherpunks like Hal Finney), molecular nanotechnology (Eric Drexler), and early AI theorists (including a teenage [[Eliezer Yudkowsky]]) debated cryonics, mind uploading, space colonization, and the technological Singularity.
3. **From Physical Transcendence to Academic Institutions**:
   - More's work directly catalyzed the subsequent formation of the World Transhumanist Association (1998) by [[Nick Bostrom]] and David Pearce, transitioning extropian ideas into Oxford academic philosophy ([[Future of Humanity Institute]]).
