# John Vervaeke

**John Vervaeke** (born 1964) is a Canadian cognitive scientist, philosopher, and Associate Professor of Psychology and Cognitive Science at the University of Toronto. He is the creator of the acclaimed 50-part lecture series *Awakening from the Meaning Crisis* (2019) and is internationally recognized for formulating the cognitive theory of **Relevance Realization**, the **4P/3V Ways of Knowing** taxonomy, and a comprehensive cognitive science of wisdom and human flourishing.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. Relevance Realization & The Frame Problem
- Formulated **Relevance Realization (RR)** as the foundational engine of intelligence and cognitive agency:
  - In any real-world environment, an agent faces an intractable **combinatorial explosion** of sensory inputs, possible inferences, and potential motor actions.
  - Classical computationalism cannot solve the **Frame Problem** through formal calculation because calculating what is relevant across an open-ended search space requires checking an infinite number of non-relevant facts.
  - True intelligence relies on **Relevance Realization**: an evolved, dynamical, bio-cost-efficient process of zeroing in on the tiny subset of information that matters for the organism's ongoing homeostatic flourishing ([[Is Consciousness Necessary for True Intelligence|Is Consciousness Necessary for True Intelligence?]]).

### 2. Consciousness as Salience Landscape Engineering
- Explains the evolutionary function of consciousness through **salience landscapes**:
  - Consciousness is not an epiphenomenal bystander or decorative monitor; it is the **qualitative, real-time foregrounding of what is relevant and backgrounding of noise**.
  - Through attentional framing, conscious awareness dynamically shapes how affordances present themselves to an embodied agent, allowing fluid, participatory navigation of ambiguous environments without algorithmic paralysis.

### 3. The 4P/3V Ways of Knowing
- Deconstructed the modern Western obsession with propositional belief by mapping four interconnected, nested levels of knowing:
  1. **Propositional Knowing**: Knowing *that* (statements, truth values, formal logic, beliefs).
  2. **Procedural Knowing**: Knowing *how* (skills, habits, motor sensorimotor routines).
  3. **Perspectival Knowing**: Knowing *what it is like to be here now* (situational awareness, salience landscapes, subjective state of presence).
  4. **Participatory Knowing**: Knowing through *mutual attunement and co-identification* (structural coupling between agent and arena, agent-arena fit).
- Modern AI systems operate almost exclusively at the **propositional and procedural** layers, while lacking the **perspectival and participatory** grounding essential for genuine wisdom and agency.

### 4. Awakening from the Meaning Crisis
- In *Awakening from the Meaning Crisis* (2019), diagnosed the modern Western epidemic of nihilism, mental health collapse, and alienation as a loss of psycho-technologies for wisdom:
  - Analyzed the historical unraveling of the nomadic hunter-gatherer sacred canopy through the Axial Age, nominalism, the scientific revolution, and secular industrialization.
  - Proposes a contemporary **Ecology of Practices** (mindfulness, dialogos, tai chi, philosophical contemplation) to cultivate relevance realization, overcome self-deception, and restore existential meaning ([[Wisdom and the Meaning Crisis|Wisdom & Cognitive Ecology]]).
