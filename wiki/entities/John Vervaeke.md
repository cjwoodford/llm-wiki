# John Vervaeke

**John Vervaeke** (born 1964) is a Canadian cognitive scientist and Associate Professor of Psychology and Cognitive Science at the University of Toronto.

He is the creator of the acclaimed 50-part lecture series *Awakening from the Meaning Crisis* (2019), renowned for developing the **4P/3V Ways of Knowing** model (Propositional, Procedural, Perspectival, Participatory) and the cognitive theory of **Relevance Realization** ([[The Meaning Crisis and Cultural Disenchantment|The Meaning Crisis & Cultural Disenchantment]]).
