# Center for Humane Technology

The **Center for Humane Technology (CHT)** is an independent non-profit organization founded in 2018 by Tristan Harris, Aza Raskin, and Randima Fernando (growing out of the *Time Well Spent* movement), dedicated to realigning technology with human nature, agency, and democratic health.

## 🛡️ Core Mission & Philosophical Framework

1. **Combating the Attention Economy**:
   - Exposed how social media algorithms create a "race to the bottom of the brainstem"—exploiting human evolutionary biases (fear, outrage, vanity) to maximize engagement, resulting in the breakdown of mental health and social trust.
2. **"The Social Dilemma" & "The AI Dilemma" (2020 / 2023)**:
   - Popularized systemic critiques of algorithmic extraction in the Emmy-winning documentary *The Social Dilemma*.
   - In *The AI Dilemma*, Harris and Raskin framed generative AI as humanity's "Second Contact" with alien algorithmic intelligence—warning that while First Contact (social media) overwhelmed human attention, Second Contact risks overwhelming human language, culture, and epistemic security.
3. **Design for Human Nature & Flourishing**:
   - Advocates for transitioning from extractive engagement optimization to architectures that protect human agency, promote [[Human Flourishing and Technology Innovation|human flourishing]], and strengthen democratic resilience.

## 🧭 Ecosystem Role & Distinctions: CHT vs. DAIR vs. AI Now

Within the landscape of critical technology organizations, CHT represents an insider design and psychological critique, distinct from structural political-economy and decolonial institutions:
- **[[Center for Humane Technology|Center for Humane Technology (CHT)]]**: Focuses on the *attention economy, persuasive design, evolutionary mismatch, and psychological vulnerability*. Its leadership emerges from Silicon Valley product teams seeking to realign interface architecture with human biology.
- **[[Distributed AI Research Institute|Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR)]]**: Founded by [[Timnit Gebru]], DAIR focuses on *intersectional bias, decolonial alternatives, labor exploitation of Global South data workers, and dismantling [[TESCREAL and The Merge|TESCREAL]] AGI hype*.
- **[[AI Now Institute|AI Now Institute]]**: Founded by [[Meredith Whittaker]] and [[Kate Crawford]], AI Now focuses on *antitrust, hyperscaler cloud cartels, competition enforcement, and state regulatory power (FTC/DOJ)*.
