# Machine Intelligence Research Institute

The **Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI)**, originally founded in 2000 as the **Singularity Institute for Artificial Intelligence (SIAI)**, is a non-profit research organization based in Berkeley, California. Founded by decision theorist [[Eliezer Yudkowsky]], Brian Atkins, and Sabine Atkins, MIRI is recognized as the world's first organization explicitly dedicated to mathematical AI alignment and mitigating existential risks from artificial superintelligence.

---

## 🏛️ Origins & Foundational Architecture

1. **Founding & "Friendly AI" (2000–2005)**:
   - Operating initially as SIAI, the institute emerged directly from Yudkowsky’s pivot away from uncritical Extropian accelerationism toward existential risk mitigation.
   - Yudkowsky recognized that superintelligent optimization algorithms would ruthlessly exploit underspecified utility functions (Goodhart's Law / specification gaming), making unconstrained self-improving seed AI inherently catastrophic for humanity unless mathematically proven to be "Friendly".

2. **The Singularity Summits (2006–2012)**:
   - SIAI organized and hosted the annual Singularity Summits, creating a high-profile platform where Silicon Valley technologists, venture capitalists, Extropians, and academic philosophers convened.
   - Speakers included Ray Kurzweil, [[Nick Bostrom|Nick Bostrom]], Aubrey de Grey, [[Peter Thiel|Peter Thiel]], and Rodney Brooks.
   - The Summits served as the primary networking hub bridging fringe transhumanist subcultures with institutional venture capital.

3. **Peter Thiel's Seed Funding (2006)**:
   - In 2006, billionaire venture capitalist [[Peter Thiel]] made his first major philanthropic donation to SIAI ($100,000, quickly scaling into seven-figure support and board advisory involvement).
   - Thiel's backing provided SIAI with institutional credibility and established the financial lifeline that allowed Yudkowsky and his collaborators to conduct full-time alignment research.

4. **The Catalyst for DeepMind (2010)**:
   - At the 2010 Singularity Summit organized by SIAI in San Francisco, Yudkowsky introduced computational neuroscientist [[Demis Hassabis]] and AI researcher [[Shane Legg]] to Peter Thiel.
   - Hassabis and Legg pitched their vision of an AGI company to Thiel, explicitly framing safety and existential risk alongside breakthrough capability. Thiel’s Founders Fund committed the crucial £1.5 million seed round, officially launching [[Google DeepMind|DeepMind]].

## 🔬 Research Paradigm: Agent Foundations & Mathematical Rigor

Unlike modern empirical AI labs that focus on post-hoc alignment techniques for neural networks (RLHF, RLAIF, mechanistic interpretability), MIRI has historically pursued **foundational mathematical alignment**:

- **Agent Foundations**: Developing theoretical models of rational agents operating under realistic resource constraints and self-reference.
- **Embedded Agency**: Formulating decision theory for agents that are physically contained within the environment they model and manipulate, rather than idealized Cartesian observers.
- **Logical Decision Theory (LDT) & Functional Decision Theory (FDT)**: Formulating decision algorithms that outperform Causal and Evidential Decision Theories in Newcomb-like dilemmas and cooperative game-theoretic scenarios.
- **Tiling Agents & Safe Self-Improvement**: Proving that an intelligent system can construct a successor agent with identical utility goals without value drift across infinite generations.

## 🛑 Pivot to Doomerism & Frontier AI Backlash (2022–2026)

1. **"We're All Gonna Die" (2022–2024)**:
   - With the explosion of large language models (LLMs) and deep learning, MIRI leadership concluded that empirical alignment techniques are insufficient to constrain superhuman systems.
   - Yudkowsky authored controversial public warnings, including a 2023 *Time* magazine op-ed advocating for international airstrikes on unauthorized data centers and a total, indefinite moratorium on frontier training runs.

2. **Analysis by Adam Becker and Cal Newport (2025–2026)**:
   - In *More Everything Forever* (2025) and on *Better Offline* (September 2026), [[Adam Becker]] and [[Cal Newport]] traced MIRI’s pivotal historical role as the transmission belt connecting 1990s Extropian mailing lists with today's frontier AI labs.
   - They noted that while MIRI’s foundational staff largely split with modern LLM scaling (viewing contemporary labs as recklessly accelerating toward doom), their eschatological framing and catastrophic risk models directly provided the founding ethos for [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]].
