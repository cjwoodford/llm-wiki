# Galen Strawson

**Galen Strawson** (born 1952) is a British analytic philosopher holding the President's Chair of Philosophy at the **University of Texas at Austin** (having previously taught at Oxford University, the University of Reading, and the CUNY Graduate Center). One of the foremost contemporary defenders of **Phenomenal Realism**, Strawson is renowned for formulating **Constitutive Panpsychism ("Realistic Monism")**—arguing that genuine physicalism logically entails panpsychism—pioneering **Cognitive Phenomenology**, and delivering the most incisive philosophical demolition of **Illusionism**.

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. Constitutive Panpsychism & "Realistic Monism" (2006)
In his landmark essay *"Realistic Monism: Why Physicalism Entails Panpsychism"* (*Journal of Consciousness Studies*, 2006), Strawson turned orthodox physicalism on its head:
- **Real Physicalism vs. "PhysicSalism"**: Strawson distinguishes *real physicalism*—the view that every real concrete thing in our universe is physical, including undeniably real human conscious experience—from *physicSalism*, the dogmatic faith that the mathematical equations of physics exhaust the entire intrinsic nature of physical matter.
- **The Epistemic Gap in Physics (Russellian Insight)**: Echoing Bertrand Russell and Arthur Eddington, Strawson notes that physics describes only the *structural, relational, and dispositional* properties of matter (mass, charge, spin—what matter *does* to other matter), remaining completely silent about matter's *intrinsic non-relational nature* (what matter *is* in itself).
- **The Impossibility of Brute Emergence**: Because experiential phenomena (pain, color, felt thought) are undeniably real physical phenomena, Strawson asks how experientiality could arise from wholly non-experiential matter. Unlike liquidity emerging from $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ molecules (where spatial-relational organization transparently yields fluid dynamics), deriving subjective qualia from 100% experiential-free stuff would be **brute emergence**—an unintelligible miracle equivalent to magic.
- **Micropsychism / Constitutive Panpsychism**: Consequently, the fundamental intrinsic fabric of the physical universe must itself be experiential (or proto-experiential). Macro-level consciousness (human, animal) is constituted by the combination and organization of micro-level experientiality woven into physical reality itself.

### 2. Demolition of Illusionism ("The Silly View")
Strawson stands at the forefront of the **Ontological Realism** battleground against [[Daniel Dennett|Daniel Dennett]] and [[Keith Frankish|Keith Frankish]]:
- **"The Silliest Claim Ever Made"**: Strawson notoriously dismissed consciousness denialism / illusionism as *"the silliest claim ever made"* in the history of human thought (*Things That Bother Me*, 2018; *"The Consciousness Deniers"*, *NYRB*).
- **The Self-Refutation of Experiential Illusion**: While illusions certainly exist regarding external objects (a bent stick in water) or theoretical interpretations, **you cannot have an illusion of conscious experience itself**. If it *seems* to you that you are experiencing excruciating pain or vivid red, then that very seeming *is* an instance of phenomenal consciousness. Consciousness is the one phenomenon where appearance and reality are identical.

### 3. Cognitive Phenomenology (*Mental Reality*, 1994)
Long before the contemporary analytic revival, Strawson's *Mental Reality* (1994) established **[[Cognitive Phenomenology and Intellectual Feeling|Cognitive Phenomenology]]** as a rigorous philosophical thesis:
- **Understanding-Experience**: Thinking, grasping a logical proof, or understanding spoken language possesses its own proprietary, irreducible phenomenal character that cannot be reduced to auditory imagery or bodily sensations.
- **The Hearing-as-Meaning Thought Experiment**: Contrasting a native French (or Russian) speaker with a non-speaker listening to the exact same acoustic broadcast proves that raw sensory inputs are identical while lived consciousness differs dramatically through the felt **phenomenology of meaning**—a direct refutation of pure syntactic computationalism (CTM).

### 4. Minimal Episodic Selfhood: "The Pearl View" (*Selves*, 2009)
In *Selves: An Essay in Revisionary Metaphysics* (2009), Strawson proposed a distinctive account of the experiencing subject:
- Rejected both the enduring Cartesian soul and narrative theories of the self, arguing instead for **thin, episodic subjects** (**SESMETs**: Subjects of Experience that are Single Mental Things).
- Like pearls strung along a thread, each momentary pulse of consciousness has a real, irreducible experiencing subject (preserving phenomenal realism against [[Thomas Metzinger|Metzinger's]] anti-realist "no-self" claim), yet these episodic selves continually arise and perish across brief psychological durations rather than persisting as a single lifelong narrative substance.
