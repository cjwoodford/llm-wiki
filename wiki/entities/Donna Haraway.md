# Donna Haraway

**Donna Haraway** (born 1944) is an American distinguished professor emerita in the History of Consciousness Department at the University of California, Santa Cruz.

Author of *"A Cyborg Manifesto"* (1985), *Simians, Cyborgs, and Women* (1991), and *Staying with the Trouble: Making Kin in the Chthulucene* (2016), Haraway is a pioneer of multispecies studies, cyborg feminism, and more-than-human relational ethics ([[More-Than-Human Kinship and Interspecies Mind|More-Than-Human Kinship & Interspecies Mind]]).
