# René Descartes

**René Descartes** (1596–1650) was a French philosopher, mathematician, and scientist regarded as the father of modern Western philosophy.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. Substance Dualism (*Res Cogitans* vs. *Res Extensa*)
- In *Meditations on First Philosophy* (1641), Descartes divided the cosmos into two fundamentally distinct substances:
  - ***Res Cogitans* (Thinking Substance)**: Immaterial, indivisible, unextended conscious mind.
  - ***Res Extensa* (Extended Substance)**: Material, divisible, spatial, mechanistic physical nature.

### 2. Mechanical Physiology & The Hydraulic Grottos
- In *Treatise on Man* (1662), inspired by the pressurized hydraulic automata in the royal water gardens of Saint-Germain-en-Laye, Descartes formulated the first comprehensive mechanical model of animal and human bodies—reducing biological functions to fluid pressure and nerve tubes ("animal spirits") ([[Machine Metaphor|The Machine Metaphor]]).

### 3. Historical Impact on the Computational Mind
- By purging nature of interiority and purpose (*anima mundi*) and restricting thought to rule-governed deduction, Cartesian rationalism laid the groundwork for the modern [[Enlightenment Conflation of Mind with Reason|Enlightenment Conflation of Mind with Reason]] and classical computationalism.
