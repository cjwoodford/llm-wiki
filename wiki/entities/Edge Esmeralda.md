# Edge Esmeralda

**Edge Esmeralda** was a month-long "pop-up city" gathering held in June 2024 in Cloverdale, California.

Convening tech founders, longevity scientists, AI researchers, and network state practitioners, it served as an experimental prototype for modern network state governance and community design ([[Network States and Tech-Feudal Governance|Network States & Tech-Feudal Governance]]).
