# Shaun Gallagher

**Shaun Gallagher** (born 1948) is an American philosopher, Lillian and Morrie Moss Chair of Excellence in Philosophy at the **University of Memphis**, and Professorial Fellow at the University of Wollongong. Widely cited across cognitive science, neuroscience, and psychiatry, Gallagher—working extensively alongside **[[Dan Zahavi|Dan Zahavi]]** and **[[Evan Thompson|Evan Thompson]]**—is a leading architect of **Phenomenological Philosophy of Mind** and **Enactivist 4E Cognitive Science**, showing how lived bodily movement, **pre-reflective self-awareness**, and social interaction shape consciousness prior to higher-order reflection.

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. Pre-Reflective Self-Awareness: Ownership vs. Agency
Together with **[[Dan Zahavi|Dan Zahavi]]** (*The Phenomenological Mind*, 2008/2020), Gallagher argues that cognitive neuroscience consistently fails to distinguish pre-reflective bodily awareness from higher-order cognitive self-reflection. Gallagher disambiguated two foundational components of the **minimal self**:
- **Sense of Ownership**: The pre-reflective feeling that *I* am the one undergoing an experience or movement (e.g., when someone pushes my arm, I still immediately feel it is *my* arm moving).
- **Sense of Agency**: The pre-reflective feeling that *I* am the initiator or author causing an action or thought (absent in involuntary movement).
- **Psychiatric & Neuroscientific Diagnostic Power**: Gallagher demonstrated that clinical anomalies such as **thought insertion** and **delusions of alien control** in schizophrenia occur precisely when the pre-reflective *sense of agency* breaks down while the *sense of ownership* remains intact—proving that minimal selfhood is grounded in motor-intentional and temporal neuro-phenomenology (anticipatory efference copy and Husserlian protention) rather than purely introspective reflection.

### 2. Body Image vs. Body Schema (*How the Body Shapes the Mind*, 2005)
In *How the Body Shapes the Mind* (2005), Gallagher resolved decades of terminological confusion across neurology, psychology, and philosophy by drawing a rigorous distinction:
- **Body Image**: A conscious system of perceptions, beliefs, and emotional attitudes directed *at* one's own body as an intentional object (e.g., looking at one's reflection or noticing pain in one's knee).
- **Body Schema**: A non-objectifying, **pre-reflective** sensorimotor system of postural habits, proprioceptive integration, and motor capacities that operates beneath conscious reflection to govern skilled movement and balance (*corps propre* in [[Maurice Merleau-Ponty|Merleau-Ponty]]).
- Drawing on clinical deafferentation cases (such as Ian Waterman, who lost proprioception and tactile feedback below the neck and had to use visual *body image* consciously to substitute for an absent *body schema*), Gallagher demonstrated how pre-reflective embodiment quietly scaffolds all conscious perception and cognition.

### 3. Front-Loaded Phenomenology & *The Phenomenological Mind*
With [[Dan Zahavi|Zahavi]], Gallagher pioneered **Front-Loaded Phenomenology** as a methodological bridge between classical phenomenology ([[Edmund Husserl|Husserl]], Heidegger, [[Maurice Merleau-Ponty|Merleau-Ponty]]) and laboratory cognitive neuroscience:
- Rather than relying exclusively on post-hoc philosophical interpretation of brain scans or training subjects in formal meditation (as in [[Francisco Varela|Varela's]] classical neurophenomenology), **front-loaded phenomenology** builds phenomenological distinctions directly *into the experimental design* of neuroimaging, behavioral, and robotics protocols.
- For instance, designing fMRI/EEG paradigms explicitly around the pre-reflective agency vs. ownership distinction allows neuroscientists to isolate neural signatures of lived subjectivity without collapsing them into third-person task reports.

### 4. Enactivist Interventions & Interaction Theory (2017, 2020)
In *Enactivist Interventions: Rethinking the Mind* (2017) and *Action and Interaction* (2020), Gallagher advanced a radical **4E / Enactivist** synthesis across philosophy of mind and predictive processing:
- **Relational Enactivism vs. Brain-Bound Internalism**: While engaging [[Karl Friston|Karl Friston's]] active inference and [[Andy Clark|Andy Clark's]] predictive processing, Gallagher critiques purely neurocentric, "skull-bound" interpretations of the predictive brain (such as treating consciousness as an intracranial "controlled hallucination" inside a Cartesian skull). Instead, predictive dynamics are continuous loops spanning brain, moving body, physical affordances, and institutional/social environments (**the socially extended mind**).
- **Interaction Theory (IT) of Social Cognition**: Rejecting standard "mindreading" theories (Theory-Theory and Simulation Theory), Gallagher argued that humans understand others primarily through **primary intersubjectivity** (direct sensorimotor resonance with facial expression, gaze, and posture from infancy) and **secondary intersubjectivity** (joint attention within shared pragmatic contexts)—never needing internal folk-psychological hypotheses to recognize mindedness in living others.
