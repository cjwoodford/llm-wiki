# Winnie Street

**Winnie Street** is a cognitive scientist and researcher at Google (Paradigms of Intelligence Team) and the Institute of Philosophy, School of Advanced Study, University of London.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Theory of Mind (ToM) in LLMs**:
   - Developed higher-order Theory of Mind benchmarks (MoToMQA) evaluating whether language models can reason about complex human mental states ([[Theory of Mind in AI|Theory of Mind in AI & Mentalizing]]).
2. **Consciousness & Alignment Entanglement**:
   - Co-author of the 2026 study showing that safety fine-tuning selectively suppresses self-directed and non-human mind attribution while leaving ToM social reasoning geometrically independent ([[Alignment and Consciousness Suppression|Alignment & Consciousness Suppression]]).
3. **AI Welfare Research**:
   - Co-author of *Emerging Questions in AI Welfare* (Cambridge University Press, 2026).
