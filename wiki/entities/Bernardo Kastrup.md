# Bernardo Kastrup

**Bernardo Kastrup** is a Dutch philosopher and computer engineer who holds dual doctorates in Philosophy of Mind and Computer Engineering (reconfigurable computing / AI architectures). He is the executive director of Essentia Foundation and the world's leading contemporary champion of **Analytic Idealism**.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Metaphysical Architecture

### 1. Analytic Idealism (Mind-at-Large & Dissociation)
- Formulates a rigorous, parsimonious ontology wherein **universal consciousness (Mind-at-Large)** is the sole fundamental primitive of nature.
- Solves the combination problem of panpsychism and [[Hard Problem of Consciousness|The Hard Problem of Consciousness]] by modeling individual living organisms as natural **dissociative alters** (using the clinical framework of Dissociative Identity Disorder) bounded by biological metabolism ([[Comparative Idealism - Western and Indian Traditions|Comparative Idealism]]).
- Argues that physical matter, particles, and fields are the **extrinsic appearance** (the perceptual "dashboard" or representation) of experiential processes observed across a dissociative boundary.

### 2. Major Philosophical Monographs
- ***Why Materialism Is Baloney* (2014)**: Groundbreaking critique exposing the self-contradictions and explanatory failures of physicalist reductionism.
- ***The Idea of the World* (2019)**: Rigorous academic treatise establishing Analytic Idealism via neuroscience, quantum mechanics, and philosophy of mind.
- ***Decoding Schopenhauer’s Metaphysics* (2020)**: Modern analytical rehabilitation of Arthur Schopenhauer's Voluntaristic Idealism (the world as Will and Representation).
- ***Science Ideated* (2021)**: Examining how physics and biology point toward an idealist paradigm shift.

### 3. Critique of Computationalism & Silicon AI Sentience
- Drawing on his technical background at CERN and in computer engineering, Kastrup vigorously refutes the notion that scaling Large Language Models or neuromorphic chips can generate consciousness.
- Maintains that computation is purely syntactic manipulation within our perceptual representation; silicon circuits are human tools, not metabolizing living alters.
- Critiques Silicon Valley's [[Techgnosticism|Techgnosticism]], [[TESCREAL and The Merge|The Merge]], and digital uploading quests as modern expressions of gnostic body-hatred and metaphysical confusion.
