# Sam Bankman-Fried

**Sam Bankman-Fried** (often referred to as **SBF**, born 1992) is the founder and former CEO of cryptocurrency exchange FTX and Alameda Research. Prior to his criminal conviction and FTX's bankruptcy in late 2022, Bankman-Fried was the premier billionaire mega-funder of the [[William MacAskill|Effective Altruism (EA)]] movement and [[Nick Bostrom|Longtermist]] existential risk mitigation projects.

---

## 🏛️ Effective Altruism & The "Earning to Give" Paradigm

1. **Conversion by Will MacAskill**:
   - As an undergraduate at MIT, Bankman-Fried met Oxford philosopher [[William MacAskill|Will MacAskill]], who convinced him to embrace utilitarian "earning to give"—the philosophy that high-earning careers in quantitative finance yield vastly more philanthropic leverage than direct non-profit work.
   - Bankman-Fried joined quantitative trading firm Jane Street Capital before founding Alameda Research and FTX, publicly pledging to donate the vast majority of his wealth to EA causes.

2. **The FTX Future Fund & Longtermist Philanthropy**:
   - In 2022, Bankman-Fried launched the FTX Future Fund, directed by MacAskill, Nick Beckstead, and Leopold Aschenbrenner, allocating grants to mitigate catastrophic threats to humanity's long-term future, primarily focusing on artificial superintelligence and biological risks.

---

## 🔬 The Anthropic Series B Funding ($500M+)

The most consequential institutional intervention of Bankman-Fried and FTX was the capitalization of [[Anthropic|Anthropic]]:
- In April 2022, Anthropic announced a $580 million Series B funding round, with over $500 million provided directly by Bankman-Fried, Caroline Ellison, and Nishad Singh.
- Bankman-Fried viewed funding Anthropic's safety-focused alternative to [[OpenAI]] as the highest-expected-value philanthropic bet available, ensuring the lab possessed sufficient compute to pioneer [[Constitutional AI and Model Welfare|Constitutional AI]].
- Following FTX’s November 2022 collapse, Anthropic's equity was later auctioned off by the bankruptcy estate for over $800 million, clearing the cap sheet for massive hyperscaler investments from Amazon and Google.

## ⚖️ Critical Examination & Cultural Impact

1. **The Culmination of the Longtermist Calculus**:
   - In *More Everything Forever* (2025) and on *Better Offline* (September 2026), science historian [[Adam Becker|Adam Becker]] and [[Cal Newport|Cal Newport]] analyzed SBF's trajectory as the catastrophic realization of Longtermist utilitarianism.
   - They argue that Bostrom's astronomical calculation ($10^{52}$ future lives) created an ethical distortion field where extreme financial risk-taking and systemic fraud were rationalized under the imperative of preventing existential extinction.
