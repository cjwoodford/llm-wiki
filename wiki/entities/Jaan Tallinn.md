# Jaan Tallinn

**Jaan Tallinn** (born 1972) is an Estonian computer programmer, investor, and philanthropist. He was one of the founding engineers of the peer-to-peer file-sharing service **Kazaa** and the voice-over-IP platform **Skype**. Tallinn is one of the most consequential early financiers of artificial intelligence safety, existential risk mitigation, and frontier AI laboratories.

Tallinn is the co-founder of both the **[[Future of Life Institute]] (FLI)** (alongside [[Max Tegmark]]) and the **Centre for the Study of Existential Risk (CSER)** at the University of Cambridge (alongside Martin Rees and Huw Price). He provided critical early seed capital to [[Google DeepMind]], was an early founding investor in [[Anthropic]], and has served as a primary philanthropic benefactor to the [[Machine Intelligence Research Institute]] (MIRI) and the [[Future of Humanity Institute]] (FHI).

## 🏛️ 1. Catalytic Role in the AI Safety Ecosystem

### 1. Seed Financing DeepMind & Anthropic
Tallinn was among the earliest tech entrepreneurs to take machine superintelligence existential risk seriously:
- **Google DeepMind**: After meeting [[Shane Legg]] through the [[Future of Humanity Institute|FHI]] and [[LessWrong]] circles, Tallinn became an early angel investor in DeepMind in 2010. His investment was explicitly motivated by Legg and [[Demis Hassabis]]'s commitment to prioritizing safety and governance alongside general capability.
- **Anthropic**: Following the 2021 schism at OpenAI, Tallinn invested in Anthropic, backing [[Dario Amodei]]'s alignment-focused Public Benefit Corporation model.

### 2. Co-Founding FLI & CSER
Recognizing that academic institutions and public policy were largely ignoring transformative technological risks, Tallinn co-founded two premier research institutions:
- **Centre for the Study of Existential Risk (CSER, 2012)**: Co-founded at the University of Cambridge alongside cosmologist Martin Rees and philosopher Huw Price to bring multidisciplinary academic rigor to the study of extinction risks from AI, synthetic biology, and climate tipping points.
- **[[Future of Life Institute]] (FLI, 2014)**: Co-founded in Cambridge, Massachusetts, with MIT cosmologist [[Max Tegmark]], creating an action-oriented NGO that organized the 2017 Asilomar AI Principles conference and the 2023 "Pause Giant AI Experiments" open letter.
