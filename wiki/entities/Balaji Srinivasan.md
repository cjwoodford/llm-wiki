# Balaji Srinivasan

**Balaji Srinivasan** (born 1980) is an American entrepreneur, investor, former Chief Technology Officer of Coinbase, and former general partner at Andreessen Horowitz.

He is the author of *The Network State: How To Start a New Country* (2022), proposing that online communities can crowdfund physical land and gain diplomatic recognition as sovereign, cloud-first nations ([[Network States and Tech-Feudal Governance|Network States & Tech-Feudal Governance]]).
