# Alfred North Whitehead

**Alfred North Whitehead** (1861–1947) was an English mathematician and philosopher who, alongside Bertrand Russell, co-authored *Principia Mathematica* (1910–1913) before developing the comprehensive system of **Process Philosophy** (or the **Philosophy of Organism**) presented in his magnum opus *Process and Reality* (1929). Whitehead's metaphysics directly challenges the mechanist, substance-based ontology that underlies modern materialism and computational functionalism, replacing static enduring objects with dynamic "actual occasions" of experience.

### 1. The Fallacy of Misplaced Concreteness & The Bifurcation of Nature
- In *Science and the Modern World* (1925) and *The Concept of Nature* (1920), Whitehead identified two fatal intellectual errors at the root of 17th-century Galilean-Cartesian science:
  1. **The Fallacy of Misplaced Concreteness**: Mistaking an intellectual abstraction for concrete reality. Modern science abstracts measurable spatial coordinates and mathematical equations from nature, and then mistakenly declares that the abstract mathematical model *is* the real world, while qualitative experience (color, sound, feeling) is an epiphenomenal illusion.
  2. **The Bifurcation of Nature**: Splitting the universe into two severed domains: an objective, physical, causally closed world of unfeeling matter in motion (*res extensa*), and a private, subjective, mental realm of qualitative experience (*res cogitans*). Whitehead showed that this artificial split made the mind-body problem—and [[Hard Problem of Consciousness|The Hard Problem of Consciousness]]—structurally unsolvable under classical physicalism.

### 2. "Actual Occasions" of Experience & Prehension (*Process and Reality*, 1929)
- Whitehead replaced the classical atomistic ontology of static material substances with an event-based ontology:
  - **Actual Entities (Actual Occasions)**: The ultimate, indivisible drops of reality are not static bits of matter, but dynamic, fleeting **occasions of experience**. Every actual occasion arises, achieves a unified perspective (**concrescence**), and perishes, becoming data for subsequent occasions.
  - **Prehension**: The mechanism of causal relation. An actual entity does not exist in isolated vacuum; it **prehends** (grasps, feels, and internalizes) the settled facts of its environment. Prehensions are affective, relational bonds that constitute the reality of each event.
  - **The Creative Advance of Nature**: Reality is fundamentally temporal, historical, and creative. It moves from settled past (*objective immortality*) toward novel, unpredetermined future occasions.

### 3. Panexperientialism vs. Computational Functionalism
- Whiteheadian process philosophy represents an **ontological breach** with [[Functionalism|Computational Functionalism]]:
  - **Functionalism as Misplaced Concreteness**: Computational functionalism takes an abstract syntactic state table or Turing transition graph and asserts that running this abstraction is identical to having a mind. To Whitehead, this is the ultimate manifestation of the fallacy of misplaced concreteness: confusing an observer-relative algorithmic model with the concrete, thermodynamic, experiential process of reality.
  - **Experientiality as Primitive (Panexperientialism)**: In process philosophy, experience is not an emergent miraculous "software trick" computed by complex silicon circuits. Low-grade experientiality (prehension) is present in the fundamental dynamics of all actual occasions. A machine composed of disconnected parts executing formal syntax is merely an aggregate (*nexus*), lacking the organic, unified concrescence necessary for authentic interiority.

### 4. Organicism & Philosophical Biology: Jonas, Levin, and Diverse Intelligence
- Whitehead’s philosophy of organism laid the foundation for 20th- and 21st-century organicism:
  - Strongly influenced **[[Hans Jonas|Hans Jonas]]**, who translated Whiteheadian process and anti-mechanist ontology into the physical reality of **metabolism** (*The Phenomenon of Life*, 1966).
  - Resonates with **[[Michael Levin|Michael Levin's]]** framework of **Diverse Intelligence** and *[[Ingressing Minds - Causal Non-Physical Patterns In-Form Embodiments|Ingressing Minds]]* (2026), where agential, purposive dynamics are multiscale patterns exerting top-down causal influence across physical systems.
