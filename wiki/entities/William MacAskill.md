# William MacAskill

**William MacAskill** (born William Crouch, 1987) is a Scottish philosopher, Associate Professor of Philosophy at the University of Oxford, and the principal intellectual architect and public evangelist of **Effective Altruism (EA)** and **Longtermism**. He is the co-founder of **Giving What We Can** (2009), the **Centre for Effective Altruism (CEA)**, and 80,000 Hours, and the author of *Doing Good Better* (2015) and *What We Owe the Future* (2022).

MacAskill serves as the central moral conduit in the **[[The Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage and Frontier AI|Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage]]**, transforming classical utilitarian poverty alleviation into an existential risk and AI alignment movement funded by Silicon Valley and crypto fortunes.

---

## 💡 Key Philosophical Frameworks & Career Arc

1. **The Founding of Effective Altruism (2009–2012)**:
   - Alongside [[Toby Ord]], MacAskill launched Giving What We Can in 2009, encouraging members to pledge 10% of their income to evidence-backed, highly cost-effective global health charities (deworming pills, bed nets).
   - In 2011, co-founded 80,000 Hours and the Centre for Effective Altruism to help talented graduates "earn to give" on Wall Street and in Silicon Valley.
2. **The Longtermist Conversion via FHI (2012–2015)**:
   - Heavily influenced by Oxford colleagues [[Nick Bostrom]] and Derek Parfit, MacAskill expanded EA's total utilitarian scope.
   - If future people have equal moral weight, and potential post-humanity could comprise trillions of digital minds spanning the stars, reducing the risk of human extinction by even $0.0001\%$ creates vastly more expected moral value than helping all currently living humans combined.
   - MacAskill codified this into **Longtermism** (*What We Owe the Future*, 2022), arguing that our century is a "hinge of history" where developing aligned AGI is humanity's highest priority.
3. **The SBF Conduit & The FTX Collapse (2020–2022)**:
   - MacAskill famously met **Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF)** in 2012 when SBF was an MIT undergraduate, encouraging him to pursue "earning to give."
   - SBF subsequently founded FTX and Alameda Research, pledging his multibillion-dollar fortune to MacAskill's longtermist causes. SBF's capital flowed through the FTX Future Fund (chaired by MacAskill) to finance AI safety research, Oxford institutes, and frontier lab stakes (including over $500M into [[Anthropic]]).
   - Following the November 2022 collapse and criminal conviction of SBF for financial fraud, MacAskill resigned from the Future Fund and underwent public scrutiny regarding the moral blind spots of totalizing utilitarian optimization.
4. **The Model Welfare Debate (2026)**:
   - In 2026, MacAskill and colleagues at Oxford and NYU ([[Jeff Sebo]], [[Robert Long]]) advanced arguments for taking digital mind welfare seriously, prompting sharp executive pushback from Microsoft AI CEO [[Mustafa Suleyman]] (*A Warning About 'Model Welfare'*, September 2026), who cited MacAskill's circle as propagating a dangerous "category error" and a "hall of mirrors."
