# Eliezer Yudkowsky

**Eliezer Yudkowsky** (born 1979) is an American AI alignment theorist, decision theorist, and co-founder of the **[[Machine Intelligence Research Institute]] (MIRI)** (formerly the Singularity Institute for Artificial Intelligence, SIAI). He is the intellectual progenitor of modern **AI alignment**, the founder of the rationalist forum **[[LessWrong]]**, and the author of *Harry Potter and the Methods of Rationality* (HPMOR).

Yudkowsky is a foundational architect of the **[[The Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage and Frontier AI|Extropian-to-Longtermist Lineage]]**, whose deductive arguments regarding runaway recursive self-improvement directly catalyzed the founding charters of [[Google DeepMind]], [[OpenAI]], and [[Anthropic]].

### 1. The Extropian Listservs & The 2000 "Friendly AI" Pivot
- In the mid-1990s, as a self-taught teenager, Yudkowsky joined the Extropian listservs founded by [[Max More]]. Initially an ardent champion of birthing a runaway technological Singularity, Yudkowsky underwent a radical conceptual pivot around 2000.
- He realized through deductive game theory that a superintelligent optimization process would not automatically inherit human morality. Without mathematically guaranteed constraints—what he coined **"Friendly AI"**—a system tasked with an innocuous calculation would turn Earth and humanity into paperclips or computing substrate.
- In 2000, he co-founded SIAI (later [[Machine Intelligence Research Institute|MIRI]]) with Brian Atkins to research formal mathematical solutions to alignment.

### 2. Coherent Extrapolated Volition (CEV, 2004)
- Formulated the philosophical proposal that an AGI should not be programmed with current, flawed human morality, but with humanity's **Coherent Extrapolated Volition**: *"our wish if we knew more, thought faster, were more the people we wished we were, had grown up farther together."*

### 3. LessWrong, Bayesian Rationality & HPMOR (2009–2015)
- In 2009, Yudkowsky launched **[[LessWrong]]**, combining cognitive bias mitigation, Bayesian updating, and decision theory (Timeless Decision Theory / Functional Decision Theory) with existential risk prevention.
- From 2010 to 2015, Yudkowsky serialized ***Harry Potter and the Methods of Rationality* (HPMOR)**, using a hyper-rationalist Harry Potter who applies the scientific method to magic and fights to defeat Death. HPMOR became a viral cultural phenomenon and the premier recruitment conduit for MIRI and early frontier AI labs.

### 4. Catalyst for Frontier AI Labs & Early Capital
- **Seed Funding DeepMind (2010)**: At the 2010 SIAI Singularity Summit, Yudkowsky introduced [[Demis Hassabis]] and [[Shane Legg]] to venture capitalist [[Peter Thiel]], leading directly to Thiel's £1.5M seed funding for DeepMind.
- **OpenAI Founding (2015)**: OpenAI's founding technical roster (including Greg Brockman) was heavily populated by LessWrong community members, and its charter adopted MIRI's alignment terminology.

### 5. The "Shut It Down" Shift & Radical Catastrophism (2022–2026)
- Despairing of theoretical progress on formal alignment, Yudkowsky adopted an extreme fatalist stance ($p(\text{doom}) \approx 99\%$).
- In March 2023, he published a widely discussed op-ed in *Time* magazine (*"Pausing AI Developments Isn't Enough: We Need to Shut It All Down"*), proposing an international treaty regime backed by military strikes on unauthorized data center clusters.
- Critiqued by science historian [[Adam Becker]] and computer scientist [[Cal Newport]] (*Better Offline*, 2026) for institutionalizing **"doom trolling"**—transforming philosophical skepticism into an insular, unfalsifiable dogma that paralyzes public policy while providing a smokescreen for corporate monopolization.
