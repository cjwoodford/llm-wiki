# Sam Altman

**Sam Altman** is the CEO and co-founder of [[OpenAI|OpenAI]] and former president of Y Combinator. He is one of the most prominent evangelists of the compute-scaling trajectory toward AGI and superintelligence.

---

## 💡 Vision & Key Concepts

1. **"The Merge" (2017)**:
   - Formulated the thesis that humanity's future is not replacement by AI, but a cybernetic and cognitive fusion with synthetic intelligence through neural interfaces and software scaffolding ([[TESCREAL and The Merge|TESCREAL & The Merge]]).
   - Argues that merging with AI represents the ultimate solution to existential risk, turning humans into a new hybrid species.

1. **Compute & AGI Scaling**:
   - Predicts rapid timelines to AGI (promising AGI by 2025–2026) and superintelligence, pitching AI as an omnipotent force to "solve all of physics" and radically disrupt the global economy ([[Scaling Laws and The Bitter Lesson|Scaling Laws & The Bitter Lesson]]).
2. **Valuation & Commercial Transformation**:
   - Led OpenAI's transition from a non-profit research lab to a for-profit commercial powerhouse valued in excess of $800 billion.
3. **Critique**:
   - Often cited in critiques of [[Techgnosticism|Techgnosticism]], the [[Apollonian Mind Virus|Apollonian Mindset]], and [[Decoloniality and Empire Technologies|Empire Technologies]] for promoting centralized techno-messianism and disembodied intelligence.
