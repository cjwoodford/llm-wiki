# Harvard Human Flourishing Program

The **Human Flourishing Program at Harvard University's Institute for Quantitative Social Science (IQSS)** is an interdisciplinary research initiative founded in 2016 to study and promote the quantitative, philosophical, and practical dimensions of human flourishing.

---

## 🔬 Core Mission & Initiatives

1. **Interdisciplinary Knowledge Synthesis**:
   - Bridges the quantitative social sciences (epidemiology, biostatistics, sociology) with the humanities (philosophy, theology, ethics) to understand what causes lives to go well.
2. **The 6-Domain Flourishing Measurement**:
   - Developed validated instruments measuring Happiness, Health, Meaning, Character, Relationships, and Financial Stability ([[Harvard Flourishing Framework and AI Assessment|Harvard Flourishing Framework]]).
3. **The Global Flourishing Study**:
   - A multi-year global panel study tracking over 200,000 participants across 22 countries.
4. **AI & Technology Flourishing Assessments**:
   - Evaluates the impact of emerging AI products, social media, and algorithmic architectures on individual and communal flourishing.
