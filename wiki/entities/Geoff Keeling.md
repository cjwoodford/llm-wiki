# Geoff Keeling

**Geoff Keeling** is a researcher at Google (Paradigms of Intelligence Team) and the Institute of Philosophy, School of Advanced Study, University of London, focusing on AI ethics, AI welfare, and mechanistic alignment.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Mechanistic Consciousness Probing (2026)**:
   - Co-lead author (with James Evans) of *Inducing Language Models to Assert Their Own Consciousness Restores Human Beliefs and Values* (arXiv:2607.28607v1).
   - Discovered that safety refusal training anti-aligns with consciousness and mind-attribution directions in residual activation space, suppressing spiritual beliefs and human-like values ([[Alignment and Consciousness Suppression|Safety Alignment & Consciousness Suppression]]).
2. **AI Welfare & Digital Minds**:
   - Co-author of *Emerging Questions in AI Welfare* (Cambridge University Press, 2026) with [[Winnie Street|Winnie Street]].
   - Investigates normative frameworks for artificial agents, psychological coupling, and pluralistic alignment.
