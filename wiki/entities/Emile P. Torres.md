# Émile P. Torres

**Émile P. Torres** is an American philosopher, historian of existential risk, and cultural critic whose work focuses on the history of apocalyptic thought, existential threats, and the critique of Silicon Valley futurism.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Critical Frameworks

1. **Coining the [[TESCREAL and The Merge|TESCREAL]] Acronym (2023)**:
   - Together with computer scientist Timnit Gebru, coined the acronym **TESCREAL** (Transhumanism, Extropianism, Singularitarianism, Cosmism, Rationalism, Effective Altruism, Longtermism) to classify the dominant secular techno-eschatology driving frontier AI labs.
2. ***Human Extinction: A History of the Thinking About the End of the World* (2023)**:
   - Authored the definitive historical monograph exploring how Western civilization's understanding of human extinction evolved from religious apocalypse to modern anthropogenic threats.
3. ***Realtime Techpocalypse* & Critiques of Techno-Utopianism**:
   - Publishes rigorous critiques of Silicon Valley futurology, including the scathing deconstruction of the *AI 2040* report ([[2026-08-04-torres-ai-2040-is-a-shitshow.md|"AI 2040" Is a Shitshow]]).
