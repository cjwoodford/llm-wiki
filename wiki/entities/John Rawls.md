# John Rawls

**John Rawls** (1921–2002) was one of the most influential political philosophers of the 20th century, best known for *A Theory of Justice* (1971) and *Political Liberalism* (1993/2005).

---

## 💡 Core Philosophical Frameworks

1. **Political Liberalism & Overlapping Consensus**:
   - Addresses how a just, stable, and free society can exist amid reasonable pluralism (deeply incompatible religious, moral, and philosophical comprehensive doctrines).
   - Introduces "political, not metaphysical" principles that can be justified through public reason without endorsing contested metaphysical doctrines.
2. **Political Conception of the Person (PCP)**:
   - Defines persons as free and equal citizens possessed of **[[Two Moral Powers|The Two Moral Powers]]**:
     1. The capacity for a **sense of justice** (fair cooperation).
     2. The capacity for a **conception of the good** (scheme of final ends).
3. **Application to AI**:
   - In modern AI ethics, Rawls' PCP is utilized by [[Seth Lazar|Seth Lazar]] and [[Ned Howells-Whitaker|Ned Howells-Whitaker]] to establish a framework for [[Artificial Personhood|Artificial Personhood]] that does not depend on biological sentience or metaphysical soulhood.
