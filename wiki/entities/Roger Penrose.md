# Roger Penrose

**Sir Roger Penrose** (born 1931) is an English mathematical physicist, mathematician, and Nobel Laureate in Physics (2020), renowned for his work in general relativity, cosmology, and the physics of consciousness.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Gödelian Argument Against AI (*The Emperor's New Mind*, 1989)**:
   - Applied Gödel's Incompleteness Theorem to prove that human conscious understanding is **non-computable** and transcends rule-based algorithms ([[Computation vs Nature and the Observer-Relative Fallacy|Computation vs. Nature & The Observer-Relative Fallacy]]).
2. **Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR)**:
   - Co-developed with Stuart Hameroff the hypothesis that quantum wave-function collapse occurring within neuronal microtubules produces conscious experience.
3. **Critique of Strong AI**:
   - Maintains that neither deterministic Turing machines nor probabilistic algorithms can ever instantiate genuine conscious insight.
