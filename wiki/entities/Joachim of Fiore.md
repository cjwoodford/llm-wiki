# Joachim of Fiore

**Joachim of Fiore** (c. 1135 – 1202) was an Italian mystic, Calabrian abbot, and biblical theologian whose prophetic exegesis fundamentally transformed the Western conception of time and history. By dividing human history into three progressive spiritual dispensations corresponding to the persons of the Holy Trinity, Joachim broke with the orthodox Augustinian view of static historical time and introduced the idea of an imminent, perfected terrestrial era. As political philosopher **[[John Gray]]** and historian **[[Norman Cohn]]** have documented, Joachim's tripartite framework served as the intellectual and structural progenitor for modern secular teleologies, revolutionary utopianisms, and contemporary techno-singularitarianism.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. The Three Ages of History (*Status*)
Joachim interpreted biblical prophecy not merely as historical record or allegorical morality, but as a progressive, dynamic pattern unfolding through three great ages:
1. **The Age of the Father (The Old Testament)**: An era characterized by the Rule of Law, fear, and servitude; dominated by the patriarchs and married laity.
2. **The Age of the Son (The New Testament)**: An era of grace, faith, and filial discipline; inaugurated by Christ and sustained by the institutional priesthood and church hierarchy.
3. **The Age of the Holy Spirit (*Tertius Status*)**: An imminent, culminating era of total spiritual illumination, universal love, and complete interior freedom. In this third dispensation, the institutional Church, sacramental hierarchy, and secular laws would wither away, rendered obsolete by direct contemplation of divine truth.

### 2. Bringing Transcendence into Terrestrial Spacetime
Prior to Joachim, orthodox Western Christian theology was dominated by **Saint Augustine’s amillennialism**:
- Augustine argued in *The City of God* that secular history (*saeculum*) is spiritually stagnant and untranscendable; the Kingdom of God is entirely transcendent, realized only in eternity after the bodily resurrection and Last Judgment.
- Joachim shattered this dualism by pulling salvation **into historical time and onto the earth**. He posited that history itself is an upward, evolutionary progression toward an era of worldly perfection, peace, and spiritual fullness prior to the final end of time.

### 3. Archetype of Secular Teleologies
As demonstrated in [[Millenarianism and Secular Eschatology]], Joachim's tripartite schema provided the secularized grammar for nearly every major modern theory of historical progress:
- **Gotthold Ephraim Lessing**: Divided history into three stages of human moral education (*The Education of the Human Race*, 1780).
- **Auguste Comte**: Formulated the "Law of Three Stages" (Theological $\to$ Metaphysical $\to$ Positive/Scientific).
- **G.W.F. Hegel**: Developed the dialectical evolution of the World Spirit (*Geist*) toward self-consciousness (Thesis $\to$ Antithesis $\to$ Synthesis).
- **Karl Marx**: Translated the Joachite pattern into materialist economics (Primitive/Feudal $\to$ Bourgeois/Capitalist $\to$ Communist Paradise). The Marxist prophecy of the "withering away of the state" is an exact secular translation of Joachim's "withering away of the institutional church."
- **Techno-Singularitarianism**: Silicon Valley's vision of history as a three-stage evolutionary rupture: Pre-technological/Biological $\to$ Industrial/Carbon Computation $\to$ Post-biological/Silicon Superintelligence (the arrival of the ASI "godhead" and cosmic post-scarcity).

### 4. The Vanguard (*Viri Spirituales*) & Midwives of the Transition
Joachim predicted that the transition into the Third Age would not occur spontaneously, but would be led by a dedicated order of spiritual monks—the *viri spirituales*—who possessed esoteric understanding of the cosmic script:
- This concept established the archetype of the **consecrated vanguard**: the enlightened elite whose special insight into historical laws entitles them to midwife the new era.
- In modern secular movements, this archetype reappears in the Jacobin Committee of Public Safety, the Leninist revolutionary vanguard, and the contemporary frontier AI rationalist community, who view themselves as uniquely ordained midwives ushering in artificial superintelligence.
