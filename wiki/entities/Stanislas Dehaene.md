# Stanislas Dehaene

**Stanislas Dehaene** (born 1965) is a French cognitive neuroscientist, professor at the Collège de France, and director of the INSERM-CEA Cognitive Neuroimaging Unit. He is the leading experimental architect of the **Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT)**.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT)**:
   - Formulated the neurobiological realization of Bernard Baars's workspace model, showing that conscious access corresponds to non-linear **"ignition"** across long-range fronto-parietal axonal pathways (~300 ms post-stimulus, P3b wave) ([[Scientific Theories of Consciousness|Scientific Theories of Consciousness]]).
2. ***Consciousness and the Brain* (2014)**:
   - Outlined empirical signatures of conscious access, distinguishing conscious perception from unconscious subliminal priming.
