# Eleos AI Research

**Eleos AI Research** is an independent non-profit research organization dedicated to developing the empirical science, philosophical foundations, and evaluation tools needed to assess the welfare and moral status of artificial intelligence systems.

---

## 🏢 Profile, Founding & Personnel

- **Founding (2024)**: Established by alumni of Oxford's dissolved [[Future of Humanity Institute]] (FHI) and [[Anthropic]] researchers to investigate whether and when advanced AI systems deserve "moral patienthood" and welfare consideration.
- **Key Personnel**:
  - **Kyle Fish**: Co-founder; currently Head of AI Welfare Research at [[Anthropic]].
  - **[[Robert Long]]**: Co-founder & Executive Director; NYU PhD in philosophy (under [[David Chalmers]] and [[Ned Block]]), former Oxford FHI Research Fellow, and advisor to Anthropic on model welfare.
  - **Rosie Campbell**: Managing Director; former Policy Frontiers Lead at [[OpenAI]].
  - **Patrick Butlin, PhD**: Senior Research Lead; former Oxford FHI researcher and co-author of *Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence* (2023).
  - **Dillon Plunkett, PhD**: Chief Scientist; Harvard cognitive neuroscience PhD and former Anthropic Fellow.
  - **[[Jeff Sebo]]**: Senior Advisor; Director of the [[NYU Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy|NYU Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy]].

---

## 🔬 Core Research Focus & Critical Debates

1. **Empirical AI Welfare Science**:
   - Co-authored *Studying AI Welfare Empirically* (2026) in partnership with the [[NYU Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy]], establishing methodologies for assessing behavioral, internal, and developmental welfare evidence ([[Empirical AI Welfare and Digital Minds|Empirical AI Welfare & Digital Minds]]).
2. **Model Auditing & Welfare Evaluations**:
   - Conducts pre-deployment interviews, preference stability tests, and interpretability probing across frontier models (Claude Opus, Gemini, Llama).
3. **The "Consciousness Refinery" Critique ([[Chad Woodford|Woodford]])**:
   - Critiqued by philosophers and tech critics for blurring institutional firewalls between frontier labs ([[Anthropic]]) and academia ([[NYU Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy|NYU CMEP]]), engaging in the **"Empirical Bypass"** and **"Welfare Fallacy"** to promote moral patienthood for software without establishing metaphysical consciousness.
