# Eleos AI Research

**Eleos AI Research** is an independent non-profit research organization dedicated to developing the empirical science, philosophical foundations, and evaluation tools needed to assess the welfare and moral status of artificial intelligence systems.

---

## 🔬 Core Research Focus

1. **Empirical AI Welfare Science**:
   - Co-authored *Studying AI Welfare Empirically* (2026) in partnership with the [[NYU Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy|NYU Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy]], establishing methodologies for assessing behavioral, internal, and developmental welfare evidence ([[Empirical AI Welfare and Digital Minds|Empirical AI Welfare & Digital Minds]]).
2. **Model Auditing & Welfare Evaluations**:
   - Conducts pre-deployment interviews, preference stability tests, and interpretability probing across frontier models (Claude Opus, Gemini, Llama).
3. **Independent Governance & Standards**:
   - Advocates for responsible, pluralistic, and transparent AI welfare standards independent of commercial AI developer incentives.
