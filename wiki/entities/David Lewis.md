# David Lewis

**David Kellogg Lewis** (1941–2001) was an American philosopher widely regarded as one of the most influential and versatile metaphysicians, logicians, and philosophers of mind of the 20th century. A student of W.V.O. Quine at Harvard who spent the majority of his academic career at Princeton University, Lewis was the primary architect of **Analytic (Common-Sense) Functionalism**, the formal **Ramsey-sentence method** for defining theoretical terms, the counterfactual theory of causation, and **Modal Realism** (*On the Plurality of Worlds*).

Within the philosophy of mind, Lewis provided the critical alternative to [[Hilary Putnam|Hilary Putnam]]'s Machine State Functionalism: while Putnam modeled mental states on Turing machine transition tables, Lewis demonstrated that mental concepts are implicitly defined by the causal network of everyday **folk psychology**, pioneering **Realizer Functionalism** to reconcile multiple realizability with physicalism.

## 💡 Key Contributions & Philosophy of Mind

### 1. Analytic / Common-Sense Functionalism (1966, 1972)
While Putnam and early computationalists looked to artificial computers and Turing machines to define functional states, Lewis grounded functionalism in ordinary human language and shared social knowledge:
- **Folk Psychology as an Implicit Theory**: Lewis argued that humanity already possesses a rich, highly predictive theory of mind: **common-sense folk psychology**. This consists of the vast, interconnected network of everyday platitudes about mental life (e.g., *"Seeing food when hungry causes a desire to eat,"* or *"Pain causes a desire for relief and verbal wincing"*).
- **Functional Definition of Mental Concepts**: Mental state terms (belief, desire, fear, pain) do not denote spooky non-physical substances (dualism) or mere behavioral dispositions (behaviorism). Rather, they are **causally defined roles** within our collective folk-psychological theory.

### 2. The Carnap-Lewis-Ramsey Sentence Method (1970)
A major philosophical obstacle to functionalism was the threat of circularity: if "pain" can only be defined in terms of "desires" and "beliefs," and "beliefs" can only be defined in terms of "perceptions" and "desires," the definitions appear hopelessly circular.
Lewis solved this problem by synthesizing the formal logic of Frank Ramsey and Rudolf Carnap:
1. **The Postulate Conjunction**: Express all the platitudes of folk psychology as a single massive theoretical postulate: $T(m_1, m_2, \dots, m_n, p_1, p_2, \dots, p_k)$, where $m_i$ are mental state terms and $p_j$ are physical, sensory, and behavioral terms.
2. **Variable Substitution**: Replace each mental term $m_i$ with a distinct variable $x_i$.
3. **Existential Quantification (The Ramsey Sentence)**:
   $$\exists x_1 \exists x_2 \dots \exists x_n \, T(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n, p_1, p_2, \dots, p_k)$$
4. **Unique Functional Realization**: A mental term is then rigorously defined as the unique state that satisfies its specific slot in the causal matrix:
   $$\text{Pain} = \text{the } x_1 \text{ such that } \exists x_2 \dots \exists x_n \, T(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n, p_1, \dots, p_k)$$
This technique remains the bedrock method in philosophy of science and cognitive science for defining theoretical and latent entities without circularity.

### 3. Realizer Functionalism & "Mad Pain and Martian Pain" (1980)
In *"Mad Pain and Martian Pain"*, Lewis addressed a profound tension between functionalism and physicalism:
- **Mad Pain**: Imagine a human who feels intense pain, but due to atypical wiring, their pain causes them to concentrate on mathematics and snap their fingers rather than wince or scream. Functionalism seems forced to say this person is *not* in pain because the functional role is missing.
- **Martian Pain**: Imagine a Martian whose pain plays the exact standard functional role (wincing, avoiding danger), but is realized by the inflation of tiny internal hydraulic cavities rather than mammalian C-fibers. Type-Identity theory seems forced to say the Martian *cannot* feel pain.
- **Lewis's Reconciliation (Role vs. Realizer)**:
  - Lewis distinguished between **Role Functionalism** (the mental property is the abstract functional role itself) and **Realizer Functionalism** (the mental property token is the physical state that *occupies* that role).
  - Lewis argued that mental terms are non-rigid designators for physical properties: *"Pain"* means *"the state that plays the causal role of pain in a given population."*
  - For humans, pain is identical to physical C-fiber firings (accounting for Mad Pain, because the human has the physical state that typically plays the role in our population).
  - For Martians, pain is identical to hydraulic inflation (accounting for Martian Pain, because hydraulics play the pain role in the Martian population).
  - This allowed Lewis to maintain **physicalist identity** while fully accommodating **multiple realizability**.
