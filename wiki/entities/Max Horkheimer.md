# Max Horkheimer

**Max Horkheimer** (1895–1973) was a German philosopher, sociologist, and director of the Institute for Social Research (the Frankfurt School).

He formulated the distinction between traditional and critical theory, co-authored *Dialectic of Enlightenment* (1944), and authored *Eclipse of Reason* (1947), critiquing how modern instrumental rationality stripped reason of objective normative purpose ([[The Commodification of Dissent and Critical Theory|The Commodification of Dissent]]).
