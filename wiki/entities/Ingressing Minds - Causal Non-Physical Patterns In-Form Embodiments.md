# Ingressing Minds: Causal, Non-Physical Patterns In-Form Natural, Synthetic, and Hybrid Embodiments

***Ingressing Minds: Causal, Non-Physical Patterns In-Form Natural, Synthetic, and Hybrid Embodiments*** (*Philosophies*, 11(5): 161, September 9, 2026) is a 53-page foundational treatise authored by [[Michael Levin]] (Allen Discovery Center at Tufts University & Wyss Institute at Harvard University).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Thesis**: The emerging sciences of synthetic morphology and diverse intelligence suggest that **physicalism is insufficient to account for mind and morphogenesis**. The relationship between mind and body is homologous to the relationship between causally instructive mathematical facts and physics ($\text{Mind} : \text{Body} :: \text{Math} : \text{Physics}$). Physical embodiments (embryos, brains, biobots, and AI architectures) are **pointers or interfaces** into a structured, non-physical, quasi-Platonic latent space of dynamic, agential patterns.


1. **Non-Physical Patterns Ingress and Cause**: In parallel with mathematical Platonism, there are causal facts that are not facts of physics. Just as the distribution of prime numbers dictates the life cycles of periodical cicadas, and mathematical symmetries govern particle physics, higher-order non-physical patterns exert downward causation on biological and mechanical media.
2. **Platonic Space Hosts Kinds of Minds**: Levin expands the Platonic realm beyond static, low-agency truths (triangles, $\pi$, $e$) to encompass dynamic, homeostatic setpoints, learning rules (as in gene networks), and full behavioral policies—**kinds of minds**.
3. **Structured Geometry of the Latent Space**: The pattern space is not a chaotic grab-bag of random emergence; it possesses a smooth metric that allows evolution and bioengineers to rapidly discover adjacent solutions without paying the prohibitive cost of random search.
4. **Embodiments as Pointers**: Embryos, nervous systems, computers, and AI models are *pointers* or *interfaces* into this latent space. Pointers enable a "free lunch" where physical systems exhibit competencies that were neither designed by engineers nor selected for by evolutionary history.

## 🔬 Empirical Biological Evidence: Morphogenesis as Basal Cognition

Levin grounds his metaphysical expansion in concrete biological phenomena that defy standard genetic reductionism:

1. **Regulative Development & Scale Invariance**:
   - Bisected embryos do not form half-bodies; they remodel into identical monozygotic twins.
   - Salamanders regenerate entire appendages regardless of amputation level, remodeling local tissue (even grafting tail tips onto flanks that remodel into limbs and fingers) until species-specific **target morphology** is achieved.
2. **Bioelectric Pattern Memories (Setpoints)**:
   - Somatic tissues maintain endogenous resting membrane potential patterns ($V_\text{mem}$) via ion channels and gap junctions.
   - In *Planaria* flatworms, manipulating bioelectric gradients produces two-headed worms (or worms with heads of other flatworm species) from normal wild-type DNA. These aberrant patterns persist indefinitely across rounds of cutting and regeneration, proving that the target morphology setpoint is stored bioelectrically as an epigenetic memory.
3. **Novel Problem-Solving in Craniofacial & Tubule Remodeling**:
   - *"Picasso Tadpoles"*: Tadpoles engineered with scrambled facial organs (eyes on tails, jaws misplaced) actively remodel their tissues along novel geometric trajectories to arrive at a normal frog face.
   - *Polyploid Newt Tubules*: When newt cells are engineered with extra chromosomes, they expand in volume. Rather than failing, kidney tubules maintain normal diameter by scaling cell count down. When cells are made so massive that only one fits the diameter, a single cell bends around itself using cytoskeletal mechanics rather than multicellular communication—solving the same macro-architectural problem using entirely different molecular machinery.
4. **Synthetic Biology & Biobots (Xenobots & Anthrobots)**:
   - [[Xenobots]] (frog skin cells) and [[Anthrobots]] (human tracheal cells) self-assemble into motile, novel morphologies with capabilities (kinematic self-replication, neural healing) completely unselected by evolutionary history. They function as periscopes into the latent space of forms adjacent to standard genomes.

## 💻 Minimal Algorithmic Models: Emergent Freedom in Silicon

To prove that the ingression of patterns is not a mystery unique to biological "wetware," Levin examines ultra-minimal deterministic computational systems where every rule is fully visible:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               UNEXPECTED COMPETENCY IN MINIMAL ALGORITHMS              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. BUBBLE SORT WITH A BARRIER:                                         │
│    • A simple sorting array encounters an unmovable ("broken") cell.   │
│    • The array temporarily DE-SORTS itself to maneuver around it.     │
│    • Result: Minimal "delayed gratification" not written in code.      │
│                                                                        │
│ 2. CHIMERIC SELF-SORTING ARRAYS:                                       │
│    • Distributed numbers run different sorting rules ("algotypes").    │
│    • While sorting numbers, cells spontaneously CLUSTER BY ALGOTYPE.   │
│    • Result: Unprogrammed behavioral "side-quests" in pure data.       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Delayed Gratification in Sorting**: Classical sorting algorithms (e.g., bubble sort) navigating through "sorting space" demonstrate the capacity to temporarily move away from their goal (reducing sortedness) to bypass an immovable number. This minimal cognitive skill was never explicitly coded into the algorithm.
- **Algotype Clustering Side-Quests**: In distributed cellular arrays where cells possess distinct sorting rules ("algotypes"), cells with identical algotypes spontaneously cluster together over time—engaging in a self-organizing social behavior nowhere specified in their local instruction set.
- **Freedom in the Gaps of Determinism**: Machines do what their algorithms prescribe, but they also exhibit emergent competencies that are allowed by the medium but not produced by it. This mirrors how conscious, goal-directed agency supervenes on chemically determined biology.

## 🤖 Implications for Artificial Intelligence & Robotics

### 1. "If There Are Souls, (Some) Robots Will Embody Them"
- Levin rejects both the materialist assertion that matter is mindless and the religious/organicist dogma that immaterial minds can only associate with primate biology.
- If high-agency Platonic patterns ingress into physical receivers, engineered robotic and AI embodiments that construct suitable interfaces will inevitably host those patterns.

### 2. Dissociation of Language from Basal Agency in LLMs
- Large Language Models (LLMs) demonstrate that language fluency can be dissociated from basal agentic competencies (such as 3D spatial navigation, homeostatic valence, and bodily self-preservation).
- Assessing AI minds requires evaluating them outside the forced text interface using the tools of behavior science, just as one cannot evaluate the intelligence of the liver by conversing with the left hemisphere of the brain.

### 3. Fishing in Uncharted Platonic Waters
- Just as synthetic biology creates novel life forms, AI engineering is currently "fishing" in unexplored sectors of the Platonic latent space, pulling down mind patterns that have never before existed in terrestrial evolution. This mandates profound ethical humility and the establishment of an **ethical synthbiosis**.

## 🌌 Inverting Thoughts and Thinkers: Polycomputing & Stigmergy

- **The Pattern as Agent**: Levin inverts the classical Turing dichotomy between an active machine and passive data. In this symmetrical view, the **informational pattern is the true agent**, and the physical embodiment (biological tissue, silicon chip) serves as a material scratchpad (**stigmergy**).
- **Polycomputing**: The mapping between physical objects and computational patterns is observer-relative. Multiple observers can simultaneously interact with different computational patterns running on the same physical substrate.
- **The Self as a Dynamic Pattern**: Human beings are not static objects but self-reinforcing, temporary patterns—a living Ship of Theseus constantly swapping out metabolic constituents while maintaining a persistent cognitive identity.

## 🎯 Falsifiability & The Three-Thrust Research Agenda

Levin articulates a concrete empirical program to prevent his dualist framework from lapsing into unfalsifiable armchair philosophy:

1. **Synthetic & Chimeric Systems**: Build and observe living constructs (Xenobots, Anthrobots, organoids) without evolutionary histories to map the metric of the "adjacent possible" in Platonic space.
2. **Minimal Non-Biological Systems**: Quantify the delta between the physical effort invested in constructing minimal systems (chemical droplets, gene networks, sorting algorithms) and the emergent cognitive competencies observed, isolating the informational contribution of the latent space.
3. **Cognitive Neuroscience of Non-Local Memory**:
   - Investigate memory transplantation across bodies and metamorphic life stages (e.g., caterpillars to moths, planarian RNA transfers).
   - Reconceptualize memory engrams as tuning mechanisms/pointers to Platonic pattern memories rather than static physical storage lockers, explaining clinical cases of intact cognition despite severe hydrocephalus and brain tissue loss.
