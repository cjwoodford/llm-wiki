# Richard Barbrook

**Richard Barbrook** (born 1956) is a British media theorist, political scientist, and Senior Lecturer in the Department of Politics and International Relations at the University of Westminster.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

### 1. *The Californian Ideology* (1995)
- Co-authored with Andy Cameron, Barbrook coined the term **The Californian Ideology** to describe the contradictory fusion of 1960s San Francisco bohemian counterculture with 1980s right-wing neoliberal libertarianism ([[The California Ideology|The California Ideology]]).
- Demonstrated that Silicon Valley's anti-statist rhetoric relies on massive historical state investments (DARPA, public universities) while promoting cyber-libertarian illusions that ignore physical labor and global inequality.

### 2. *Imaginary Futures* (2007)
- Explored the political history of the Cold War origin of computing and how utopian visions of artificial intelligence and the "information society" serve as ideological instruments of geopolitical dominance.
