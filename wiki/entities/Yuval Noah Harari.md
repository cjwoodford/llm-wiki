# Yuval Noah Harari

**Yuval Noah Harari** (born 1976) is an Israeli historian, philosopher, and bestselling author of *Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind* (2014), *Homo Deus: A Brief History of Tomorrow* (2016), and *21 Lessons for the 21st Century* (2018).

Harari is one of the most influential public intellectuals in Silicon Valley and global technocratic circles (including the World Economic Forum), known for popularizing the concept of **Dataism** and framing human history through evolutionary reductionism.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Critiques

### 1. The Dataist Worldview (*Homo Deus*)
- Formulated the philosophy of **Dataism**: the belief that the entire universe consists of data flows and that any organism (including humans) is simply a biochemical data-processing algorithm.
- Argued that as non-conscious external algorithms (AI, Big Data) surpass human cognitive processing, human individual agency, free will, and humanism will be superseded.

### 2. The Critique: "Mr. Left Hemisphere"
- Cultural critics and cognitive philosophers (e.g. [[Iain McGilchrist|Iain McGilchrist]]) point to Harari as the archetype of **Left-Hemisphere Hyperrationality**:
  - Harari reduces all human culture, religion, art, and meaning to "fictional stories" (*intersubjective myths*) with zero ontological reality.
  - Collapses qualitative consciousness into functional algorithmic calculation, ignoring the [[Hard Problem of Consciousness|Hard Problem]] and mistaking mathematical modeling for living existence ([[The Divided Brain and Hemispheric Lateralization|The Divided Brain]]).
