# Tyler VanderWeele

**Tyler J. VanderWeele** is the John L. Loeb and Frances Lehman Loeb Professor of Epidemiology at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and Director of the [[Harvard Human Flourishing Program|Human Flourishing Program]] at Harvard University.

---

## 💡 Key Contributions & Ideas

1. **The Comprehensive Flourishing Measure**:
   - Developed the widely validated 12-item empirical measure of human flourishing spanning 6 core domains: Happiness, Health, Meaning, Character, Relationships, and Financial Stability ([[Harvard Flourishing Framework and AI Assessment|Harvard Flourishing Framework]]).
2. **The Global Flourishing Study**:
   - Leads a landmark longitudinal study tracking 200,000+ individuals across 22 countries to identify the determinants of human flourishing.
3. **Flourishing Considerations for AI (2026)**:
   - Co-authored *Flourishing Considerations for AI* with Jonathan Teubner, establishing that AI systems must be evaluated by whether they support intrinsic human goods and calling for the discontinuation of predatory relational chatbots.
