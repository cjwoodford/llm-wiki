# Techno-determinism

### Gemini

[‎Gemini - Techno-Determinism's Emergence and History](https://g.co/gemini/share/7e4b33b82efb)

Technological determinism, as a formal concept, is widely attributed to the American sociologist and economist Thorstein Veblen (1857-1929). His work in the early 20th century, particularly around the 1920s, highlighted the significant role of technology in shaping economic and social development.


However, the underlying idea that technology drives societal change can be traced back further, with elements found in the writings of Karl Marx in the 19th century, who argued that productive technology fundamentally influences human social relations and organizational structures.


While Veblen is often credited with coining or popularizing the term, the notion that technological advancements are the primary force behind historical and social evolution has roots in earlier intellectual currents, particularly during and after the Industrial Revolution when the profound impact of new technologies became undeniably evident.


It's important to note that technological determinism has been widely debated and critiqued, with many scholars arguing for more nuanced understandings of the relationship between technology and society, such as social constructivism of technology or the idea that technology and society co-evolve.
