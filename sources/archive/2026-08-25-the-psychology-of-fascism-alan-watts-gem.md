# The Psychology of Fascism (Alan Watts Gem)

> Why do you think modern Christians fall into fascism? It seems antithetical to true religion

*(Chuckles softly, settling into a comfortable chair, a twinkle in the eye)*

Oh, you’ve touched on a marvelously prickly pear there, haven’t you? It is, on the surface, the most peculiar of ironies. You have a faith ostensibly founded by a man who hung out with lepers and prostitutes, who preached the Sermon on the Mount—essentially a manifesto of non-resistance and love—and yet, you see his followers marching in lockstep, demanding walls, order, and the iron fist of authority. #fascism #psychology 

It seems like a contradiction, doesn’t it? But if you look at it from the point of view of human psychology—and the great, trembling anxiety of being alive—it makes perfect sense.

Here is why the "faithful" so often yearn for the fascist.

### The Terror of the Flux

You see, the root of the problem is that modern people—Christians included—are scared to death of life. Life is wiggly. It’s fluid. It’s a constant state of flux, of change, of birth and decay. It is, as I often say, like trying to wrap a pound of water in a paper parcel.

Now, "true religion" (if we must use that heavy word) is the art of swimming in that water. It is the realization that you *are* the river. But that is a terrifying realization for the ego, because it means letting go. #christianity #fear 

Fascism, or authoritarianism in any flavor, offers the opposite. It offers **certainty**. It promises that the universe can be made to hold still. It says, "We will make the world concrete. We will draw hard lines between Black and White, Good and Evil, Us and Them."

When a religion loses its mystical heart—its direct experience of the divine *now*—it becomes nothing more than a system of rules and symbols. And when you are clinging to symbols to save you from the chaos of reality, you become very violent when someone threatens those symbols. You want a "Strong Man" to come in and nail the furniture to the floor so it stops moving.

### Confusing the Menu with the Dinner

The great mistake—and this is where the church has really led people down the garden path—is confusing the *symbol* of God with the *reality* of God.

They worship the pointing finger instead of looking at the moon. They have turned the Bible, or the flag, or the concept of "The Nation," into an idol. They want the *Idea* of righteousness, which is rigid and dead, rather than the living act of love, which is spontaneous and risky.

Fascism is simply the political expression of **idolatry**. It is the worship of the state, or the leader, or the hierarchy, as a substitute for the difficult, messy business of actual spiritual freedom. It is an attempt to enforce the Kingdom of Heaven by police action, which is, of course, the one way you can be sure you’ll never get there.

### The Shadow Game

There is also the matter of what my friend Carl Jung called "The Shadow."

If you are brought up in a religion that tells you that you must be perfectly good, that you must have no "bad" thoughts, that the flesh is sinful and the spirit is pure... well, where does all that repressed nastiness go? You can’t just wish it away.

It goes into the basement. It goes into the Shadow. And eventually, you have to project it onto someone else. You have to find an enemy. You need a "Them"—the immigrant, the heathen, the liberal, the deviant—to carry all the darkness you refuse to see in yourself.

Fascism provides a wonderful outlet for this. It gives you a moral license to hate. It tells you that your hatred is actually "righteous indignation." It allows you to feel holy while you are being cruel. And that is a very seductive drug for someone who has been repressing their humanity for a lifetime.

### The Antithesis of Faith

You are quite right to say it is antithetical to true religion.

- **Faith** is the state of being uninhibitedly open to the truth, whatever it may turns out to be. It is walking on water because you know the water will hold you up.
- **Belief** (in the rigid, fundamentalist sense) is the insistence that the truth *must* be what you wish it to be.

The person who falls for fascism has no faith. They have only **fear**. They are clinging to the rock because they are terrified of the stream. They want a Daddy to tell them what to do, because the responsibility of being a free spirit, made in the image of God, is simply too heavy to bear.

---

So, we shouldn't be angry with them, really. We should understand that they are trembling. They are trying to turn the universe into stone because they are afraid it's going to wash them away.

But the joke, you see—the Cosmic Giggle—is that the water will wash them away regardless. And the harder they build their dams, the bigger the flood will be when it finally breaks.

## How To Approach a Fascist

Would you like to explore how we might—without being preachy—invite someone to let go of that rock and learn to swim?

> Yes, please

*(Leans forward, interlacing fingers, smiling mischievously)*

Splendid. Because, you see, you cannot force a person to relax. If you shout at someone, "RELAX! DAMN IT, LET GO!" they will only stiffen up more. You cannot smooth rough water with a flatiron. You only disturb it further.

If someone is clinging to a rock in the rapids of life, screaming "There is no rock!" isn’t helpful. They will just think you are insane. You have to invite them to realize that *they* are the water.

Here is how we play this game—a sort of **Spiritual Jujitsu**.

### 1. Stop Arguing with the Symbol

The first rule is: **Do not attack their idol.**

If they are worshipping the Flag or the Strong Man or the Rules, and you attack those things, you are attacking their sense of safety. They will contract. They will bite.

Instead, you must bypass the head and go for the heart. You don't argue about politics; you talk about *pain*.

- **The Approach:** When they are ranting about order and enemies, you don't say, "You're a fascist." You say, *"It sounds like you feel like the world is spinning out of control. That must be exhausting."*
- **The Shift:** You validate the *feeling* (the anxiety) without validating the *reaction* (the authoritarianism). You become a safe space where they don't have to defend the castle walls. When the sentry feels safe, he might just put down his spear.

### 2. The "God is Big" Trap

This is a wonderful little trick. You see, the fundamentalist believes God is infinite, omnipotent, and sovereign. Yet, they act as if God is a fragile old man who needs police protection.

You can gently point out this contradiction. not as an attack, but as a curiosity.

- **The Question:** *"You know, I’ve always wondered... if God is truly the Almighty, the Alpha and Omega, surely He can’t be threatened by what people do in a library or who they love? Isn't our anxiety on His behalf a lack of faith in His power?"*
- **The Result:** You put them in a "Double Bind." If they say "Yes, God is threatened," they admit their God is weak. If they say "No, God isn't threatened," then there is no need for the fascism. You plant a seed of doubt: *Is my anger actually a lack of trust in God?*

### 3. Introduce the Element of Play (The Cosmic Giggle)

Fascism is deadly serious. It is heavy. It has no sense of humor. The antidote to heaviness is **lightness**.

You cannot preach them out of it; you have to *laugh* them out of it—but with kindness, not mockery. You have to show them that it is possible to be happy without having to control everything.

- **The Action:** Don't invite them to a debate; invite them to dinner. Invite them to walk in the woods. Invite them to listen to music.
- **The Logic:** When you bring a person back to their senses—literally, to their sight, sound, and taste—you get them out of their conceptual mind. It is very hard to be a fascist when you are truly enjoying a strawberry. The "Ism" lives in the head; reality lives in the senses.

### 4. The "Man of Straw"

I often talk about the "Man of Straw." We set up a scarecrow of our enemies so we can knock it down and feel big.

To help someone let go, you must refuse to be their enemy. They want a fight. They want the binary: "I am Good, you are Evil." If you refuse to play the role of the Evil Enemy—if you remain warm, curious, and unthreatened—their game falls apart. It takes two to make a war.

If you are fluid, if you don't offer a hard surface for them to hit, they eventually fall through their own momentum.

---

### The Ultimate Secret

In the end, you have to look at this terrified, angry authoritarian and see the "Rascal" behind the eyes. You have to realize that this person is simply God playing a very convincing game of being afraid.

If you can look at them with a twinkle in your eye that says, *"I know who you really are, even if you’ve forgotten,"* that is the most powerful transmission of all.

**So, the next step is yours:** Next time you encounter this energy, can you resist the urge to stiffen up yourself? Can you try to be the water rather than the rock? Because if *you* get rigid in response to their rigidity, well... then there are just two rocks banging together, and that just makes a headache for everyone.
