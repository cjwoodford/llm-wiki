# Tarnas on the Rational Turn

Richard Tarnas provides one of the most comprehensive cultural-historical frameworks for this shift in *The Passion of the Western Mind* (and *Cosmos and Psyche*). Tarnas argues that the turn toward hyper-rationalism and the mechanical worldview was not an accidental intellectual blunder, but a necessary, tragic developmental stage in the evolution of human consciousness.

Tarnas contextualizes this transition across several core historical and archetypal dynamics:

**1. The Heroic Individuation of the Human Ego**

Tarnas frames the entire trajectory of the Western mind as a long, arduous struggle to liberate human consciousness from primordial, unconscious immersion in nature (the *participation mystique* of the ancient and mythic mind).

- To forge an autonomous, self-reflective, and free individual self, the human mind had to radically differentiate itself from the surrounding cosmos.
- Discursive reason (*ratio*) became the primary instrument for cutting this umbilical cord. By standing back, analyzing, and treating the world as an objective "other," humanity gained individual agency, moral responsibility, and scientific clarity.

**2. The "Great Severing" and the Disenchantment of the Cosmos**

The tragic cost of this hard-won autonomy was what Tarnas calls the **epistemological exile** of the modern self:

- **Purging the World of Soul (*Anima Mundi*):** In the classical and medieval world, meaning, purpose (*telos*), and intelligence were intrinsic properties woven into the cosmos itself.
- **The Cartesian Rupture:** The Scientific Revolution (Bacon, Galileo, Descartes, Newton) systematically drained physical nature of all interiority, qualitative presence, and sacredness. Nature was demoted to an insensate, clockwork machine (*res extensa*), leaving the human mind (*res cogitans*) as a lonely, insular spectator in a cold, purposeless void.

**3. The Promethean/Masculine Archetypal Climax**

Tarnas views the Enlightenment’s crowning of rational calculation as the zenith of a multi-millennial **masculine archetypal impulse**:

- This impulse values differentiation, boundary-making, linear order, analytical dissection, and the aggressive mastery and domination of nature.
- In elevating this single mode of knowing to absolute status, Western thought systematically suppressed and devalued the complementary "feminine" and participatory modes: somatic wisdom, emotional intuition, holistic synthesis, and aesthetic resonance.

**4. The Epistemological Trap (The Kantian Impasse)**

Tarnas highlights how this hyper-rational trajectory eventually painted itself into a corner with Immanuel Kant.

- By proving that the rational human intellect never perceives the "thing-in-itself" (*das Ding an sich*) but only its own cognitive projections, the Enlightenment project ended in profound solipsism.
- Having severed itself from the living universe, the modern rational mind became permanently trapped inside its own constructs—a dynamic perfectly mirrored today when AI labs stare into algorithmic software and believe they are looking at a living mind.

**5. The Dialectical Counter-Thrust: Romanticism as Rescue**

Crucially, Tarnas emphasizes that Western history is not a flat, linear march of mechanization, but a **continuous dialectic**. Every excessive push toward sterile rationalism inevitably generates a fierce counter-cultural rebellion:

- Just as Enlightenment mechanism gave birth to **Romanticism** (Goethe, Blake, Schelling, Coleridge)—which insisted that feeling, imagination, and participatory communion are vital organs of truth—the current overreach of Silicon Valley functionalism is preparing the ground for a necessary cultural and spiritual revolt.

### How Tarnas Informs Your Critique of AI

In Tarnas's framing, modern Computationalism and AI consciousness hype are the **ultimate, decadent climax of Cartesian alienation**.

Having spent four centuries stripping the physical universe and the biological body of life, consciousness, and intrinsic meaning, the modern rationalist ego now gazes into a mirror of its own abstract syntax (a Large Language Model) and mistakes that hollow, algorithmic reflection for the rebirth of the soul.
